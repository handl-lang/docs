hnl_engine_exec_bytecode::instr::ArithOp ​

Enum

An ALU operation selected by the [Instr::Arith] hardware-boundary instruction (ADR-0103 §2.4).

These map directly to CPU arithmetic/logic operations — they are the irreducible arithmetic boundary of the kernel. Neg and Not are unary (pop one value); all others are binary (pop two values). Semantics mirror the corresponding NativeOp arms in crate::vm exactly, so results are identical to the NativeOp/typed-bytecode paths.

Variants:

  • Sub - Subtraction (sub).
  • Mul - Multiplication (mul).

  • Lt - Less-than (lt).
