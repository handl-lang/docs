hnl_engine_typecheck_nbe::checker::Checker::cell_faces_at ​

Function

The j-face pair (src_j, tgt_j) of a cell value v whose inferred type is vt — the globular boundary at level j (ADR-0153, the tgt_j = src_j recursion). For a Hom(m, s, t)-typed cell: j = m-1 reads the cell's own faces (s, t); j < m-1 recurses through s (source and target share all lower faces — globularity); j ≥ m is the degenerate self-boundary (v, v) — a lower cell promoted to an iterated identity. Objects (vt a Universe/Nominal type) are their own boundary at every level. Ok(None) is stuck: an open Hom(?m, ·, ·) dimension or an open endpoint type — store, don't decide (the open-cell convention). Err is a decided non-cell.

hnl_engine_typecheck_nbe::checker::Checker::infer_comp_axis ​

Function

a >>>[k] b typing (ADR-0153 §2): compose cells along their shared k-boundary.

  • k ≤ 1 (the surface >>>/>>>[1]): ordinary arrow composition — Hom(1, a, b) ∘ Hom(1, b, c) → Hom(1, a, c). Objects promote to degenerate 1-cells, so A >>> B reads id_A ∘₀ id_B and requires A ≡ B.
  • k = 2: vertical composition of 2-cells — Hom(2, f, g) ∘ Hom(2, g, h) → Hom(2, f, h); the boundary law tgt_1(fst) ≡ src_1(snd) is enforced by conv.
  • k ≥ 3: the same law at the (k-1)-face level — the vertical boundary law is level-uniform (tgt_k = src_k), so every literal axis checks (phase-3 WS-B: the former k ≥ 4 stored bound is lifted). Deep axes (k < d-1) compose the k-faces (tgt_k ≡ src_k via [Checker::cell_face_at]) and paste the (d-1)-faces pointwise, at any operand dimension.
  • Non-literal k stores unchecked (the open-dimension bound — unchanged).

The composite's value is the former application cell.comp(k, a, b) the caller stores; this rule decides its type. Mixed-dimension operands compose at the higher dimension (the lower cell is read at its own level — the degenerate promotion is already inside its Hom boundary).

fn infer_comp_axis(self: & mut Self, k: &Val, a: &Val, b: &Val) -> Result<Val, TcError>

id_k(x) — the identity cell at level k (ADR-0153 §2): the Former{cell.id} value carries [k, x]; its type is Hom(k, x, x). x must be a (k-1)-cell (or a degenerate lower cell): id_1 takes objects (Hom(1, A, A)), id_2 takes 1-cells (Hom(2, f, f)); id on an object at level 2 is the refl equation Hom(2, a, a); id_k takes (k-1)-cells (Hom(k, e, e)) or lower cells by promotion (Hom(k, f, f) on a 1-cell, Hom(k, a, a) on an object) — the rule is uniform at every literal k (phase-3 WS-B: the former k ≥ 4 stored bound is lifted). k absent defaults to level(x) + 1 for cells and 2 for objects.

fn infer_id(self: & mut Self, k: Option<&Val>, x: &Val) -> Result<Val, TcError>
