The substrate-free kernel ​

The game kernel knows nothing about e-graphs, tensors, or laws. That is a contract, not an accident: gamekit.engine, gamekit.scoring, gamekit.calibrate, gamekit.advantage, gamekit.gamedata, and gamekit.players import nothing outside the stdlib — a gamekit ⊥ eqsat edge enforced by the repo's import-linter contract.

Why the wall exists ​

  • A board is a protocol, not a library. observe() -> state and step(action) -> (state, report) is all the drivers need. Anything with two methods can be a board — including a hand-rolled toy that exercises every driver path in the kernel's own suite.
  • Players bind the domain lazily. Each player folder ships a make arm factory that imports mlopt_discovery/domain machinery inside the factory — so import gamekit.players stays stdlib-only, and in this repo (where no domain substrate is installed) the factories decline honestly instead of failing at import.
  • register_player(spec, make) is the plugin seam. A downstream domain seats its boards through the registry: a fresh name supplies make; a name matching the shipped roster unions boards into the shipped seat.

The referee seam ​

The split that keeps the game honest runs through gamekit's edge:

  • the value spec is gamekit's — ScoreGate/ScoringSpec declare the axes and thresholds (speed, memory, launch, params, win-rate) as data; score(spec, measured) consumes a plain Mapping[str, float] and returns gates cleared, the weighted value, and the axes the measurement lacked;
  • the measurement is the gauntlet's — the measured numbers come from the admission machinery on the substrate/domain side (the gauntlet).

The two sides meet only through that mapping: measurement code never imports the scoring module, and the scoring module imports no measurement machinery. Calibration (gamekit.calibrate — Platt and isotonic fits plus metrics) tunes scores, never verdicts.

Where the rest lives ​

The episode machinery, the player roster, and the measured records are in the reference; the cross-cutting narrative — why a game at all, the learned-policy loop, the meta-game — is the game on the graphopt side.
