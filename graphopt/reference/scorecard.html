Per-case detail ​

biased_chain_fold — Two-layer biased Linear chain → affine compose ​

linear(linear(x,A,b1),B,b2) → (BA)x + (B·b1+b2) — the affine-map composition assoc_linear_bias, the biased sibling of weight_chain_fold's assoc_linear. The composed member spells the fused bias linear(x, BA, B·b1) + b2 so both B-products stay well-typed for every hidden width and fold at compile time — one GEMM plus one broadcast add at runtime.

  • signals: law:assoc_linear_bias
  • baselines: eager two-GEMM (self) 4,098; pre-folded affine (ref) 2,049 — one Linear carrying W=B·A, b=B·b1+b2; inductor (compile) — no freeze — Inductor cannot fold the weights

blelloch_scan — Unrolled first-order recurrence → parallel scan ​

Blelloch 1990, "Prefix sums and their applications" — the sequential recurrence h_t = a·h_{t-1} + x_t re-expressed as a parallel prefix scan. The spelled fixture unrolls the recurrence over T steps; the engine's diagonal-affine monoid lift (decay_state_lift, SCAN_DIAG_LAWS) turns it into the aff_diag/applyd carrier — the same monoid RetNet's chunked scan runs on.

  • signals: law:decay_state_lift, op:applyd
  • baselines: eager unrolled (self) 759; cumsum prefix (ref) 1,494 — h_t = P_t · cumsum(x_t/P_t) — the closed-form parallel scan; inductor (compile) — measured-only (no symbolic form)

conv_bn_fold — Conv2d + eval BatchNorm2d fold ​

The standard inference fold — PyTorch's torch.nn.utils.fuse_conv_bn_eval. In mlopt it is conv_bn_fold, the first verbatim machine-minted composite law (depth-4 syn_* chain shipped through mlopt_core.laws.minted; project/retros/promoted-laws.md). The spelled fixture is the eval-mode BatchNorm2d(Conv2d(x)) site the mint saw (tests/test_minted_laws.py).

  • signals: law:conv_bn_fold
  • baselines: eager (self) 29,186; hand-folded conv (ref) 27,649 — PyTorch fold recipe — one conv, BN absorbed into W'/b'; inductor (compile) — measured-only (no symbolic form)

cumsum_scan — Unit-decay recurrence → aff carrier (cumsum association) ​

The a = 1 corner of the diagonal-affine monoid — h_t = h_{t-1} + x_t unrolled is a prefix sum, and affd_lift_unit / affd_lift_unit_step raise it to applyd(aff_diag(h, x), 1) under carrier_search. Derives-but-loses at benchmark scale, recorded honestly. The lift itself is sound and extraction works as designed: _carrier_upgrade selects the fully-carried member because the delivered-batched model prices it below generic — a win the uncalibrated constants promise but the wall clock denies (full lane: the batched delivery runs ~3× slower than the unrolled adds; at T=32 the level machinery's per-call cost outweighs 31 serial dispatches — a cost-model fidelity gap, and delivered_cost_for with a measured TargetProfile is the correction channel). The exec axis reports the uniform generic-model price (+25.7 %); the member ships batched, a whole-spine property dag_cost cannot express. The cumsum ref floor (34,940) stays unreachable — no law produces the cumsum op, a coverage gap, not a bad law.

  • signals: law:affd_lift_unit, law:affd_lift_unit_step, op:applyd
  • baselines: eager unrolled (self) 1,115,257; cumsum prefix (ref) 34,940 — x.cumsum(1)[:, -1] — the practitioner's parallel-scan spelling; inductor (compile) — measured-only (no symbolic form)

flash_attention — Hand-spelled softmax attention → fused SDPA ​

Dao et al. 2022, FlashAttention — attention without materialising the (T,T) score matrix. The spelled fixture computes softmax(q@kᵀ/√d + causal-mask) @ v by hand; the sdpa_fold_* family folds it to the fused sdpa op and the causal-mask specialization closes it to is_causal (DEFAULT rules).

  • signals: law:sdpa_fold_adddiv
  • baselines: eager spelled (self) 33,544; F.scaled_dot_product_attention (ref) 32,772 — the fused kernel PyTorch ships; inductor (compile) — measured-only (no symbolic form)

glu_fold — chunk·σ(chunk) manual GLU → fused glu kernel ​

Dauphin et al. 2017, "Language Modeling with Gated Convolutional Networks" — a ⊗ σ(b) over the two halves of the last dim IS F.glu. The corpus carried the kernel image but no manual spelling until ManualGluMLP closed the pair (glu_fold, corpus-expansion-r2.md §6); the parity-of-split-axis guard keeps the odd-extent corner from minting an glu that cannot denote.

  • signals: law:glu_fold
  • baselines: eager spelled (self) 98; F.glu (ref) 33 — the fused kernel; inductor (compile) — measured-only (no symbolic form)

gqa_absorb_repeat — Materialised repeat_kv → sdpa enable_gqa ​

The llama2.c GQA spelling: unsqueeze→expand→reshape copies each kv head r times before F.sdpa — a materialised copy diagonal. SDPA's enable_gqa flag implements the same broadcast inside the kernel, and gqa_absorb_repeat pushes the copy into the consumer: the duplicated heads are never materialised. (Regression: the law's LHS pinned legacy arg4/arg5 attr metavars the canonical ATTR_SCHEMA boundary never emits — zero fires until the pattern read dropout_p/is_causal.)

  • signals: law:gqa_absorb_repeat
  • baselines: eager spelled (self) 199,688; F.sdpa enable_gqa (ref) 196,612 — the kernel-side head broadcast PyTorch ships; inductor (compile) — measured-only (no symbolic form)

mha_qkv_fuse — Split QKV projections → one widened GEMM ​

The fused-QKV transform Megatron-LM's MergedColumnParallelLinear performs by hand and TFLite's fuse_qkv pass does structurally: <q,k,v> : X -> V³ is ONE map into the product space — three bias-free projections of one input concat into a single (3d,d) GEMM plus three free split views. qkv_fuse/qkv_fuse_asym are pairing-subsumed in the bank (tags SUBSUMED, kept for the cache); the shipped reach is the non-local pairing pass — paired_extract is the honest signal that the forced shared-GEMM term won.

  • signals: stat:paired_extract
  • baselines: eager three-GEMM (self) 32,772; merged QKV (ref) 32,770 — one (3d,d) Linear + chunk — the hand-fused impl; inductor (compile) — measured-only (no symbolic form)

mul_unsq_nat — Two-sided unsqueeze naturality ​

mul_unsq_nat — the first law minted from a corpus op-tuple (DeltaRuleBlock's β·k): unsq(u,d)·unsq(v,d) → unsq(u·v,d), guard resolved by the adversarial sweep (project/retros/mul-unsq-nat.md; provenance MACHINE_SHIPPED). Measured −12.27% on its source workload.

  • signals: law:mul_unsq_nat
  • baselines: eager spelled (self) 75; mul-then-unsqueeze (ref) 50; inductor (compile) — measured-only (no symbolic form)

retnet_chunkwise — RetNet chunkwise retention carrier ​

Sun et al. 2023, "Retentive Network" — the chunkwise-recurrent form of retention trades the (T,T) decay-masked score matrix for a decayed cross-chunk state. The spelled fixture is the intake zoo's _ChunkedRetention ((QKᵀ ⊙ D)V, decay built in-graph); the engine lifts it to the om_decayed carrier — measured parity with the paper's own chunked impl, faster under graph replay (bench/drivers/probes/retnet_baseline_probe.py).

  • signals: law:om_decayed_lift, op:om_decayed
  • baselines: eager quadratic (self) 761,863; paper chunkwise (ref) 491,656 — Sun et al. 2023 chunkwise recurrence, reimplemented; inductor (compile) — measured-only (no symbolic form); FLA chunk_retention (external) unavailable — Triton kernel — scratch dep; measured ~7-11x ahead of the carrier

rms_norm_bridge — Misspelled RMSNorm → fold via canonicalizer bridges ​

The recorded-miss spellings of rms-norm-law.md, folded: the corpus spells x² as mul(x, x) and the reciprocal root as pow(·, -0.5) — rms_norm_fold pins pow(u, 2) and rsqrt(·) literally, so the fold only reaches this graph through the canonicalizer bridges mul_square → square_to_pow and pow_to_rsqrt (tests/test_square_rsqrt_laws.py). A real corpus fragment reads exactly this way — normalizer code is written x*x + **-0.5 far more often than pow(2) + rsqrt.

  • signals: law:rms_norm_fold, law:mul_square, law:pow_to_rsqrt
  • baselines: eager spelled (self) 218; F.rms_norm (ref) 129 — the fused kernel PyTorch ships; inductor (compile) — measured-only (no symbolic form)

rms_norm_fold — Manual RMSNorm → fused rms_norm kernel ​

Zhang & Sennrich 2019 RMSNorm, popularised by LLaMA (Touvron 2023): x·rsqrt(mean(x²)+eps)·w IS F.rms_norm spelled by hand. In mlopt it is rms_norm_fold, the fourth machine-discovered law admitted to the bank — measured −83% on RMSNorm / −31% on TransformerBlock extracted cost (project/retros/rms-norm-law.md). XLA/TFLite and Transformer Engine ship the fused kernel as a named primitive; Inductor does not pattern the whole reduce-scale chain back to it.

  • signals: law:rms_norm_fold
  • baselines: eager spelled (self) 282; F.rms_norm (ref) 129 — the fused kernel PyTorch ships; inductor (compile) — measured-only (no symbolic form)

rms_norm_nogain — Gain-free manual RMSNorm → fused rms_norm ​

The weight-free twin of the RMSNorm fold — x·rsqrt(mean(x²)+eps) with no channel gain is F.rms_norm(x, ns, weight=None). It is the gained fold's inner mul, so rms_norm_fold_nogain fires inside every gained site too (the cost model picks the fused member either way; project/retros/rms-norm-law.md).

  • signals: law:rms_norm_fold_nogain
  • baselines: eager spelled (self) 217; F.rms_norm (no weight) (ref) 65 — the fused kernel, weight=None; inductor (compile) — measured-only (no symbolic form)

sdpa_decomposed — Fused SDPA — decompose, migrate the scale, refold ​

The fused-op blindness: F.scaled_dot_product_attention is opaque to the e-graph — no law can reach inside the kernel (the measured 0.80–0.88× vs compiled on BiRefNet/pointwise-fusion rows, docs/results.md). sdpa_expand_s mints the kernel's own spelled form softmax(q@kᵀ·s)@v; inside, the score-scale migration (naturality_scalar_left_rev → scale_in_{transpose,reshape} → linear_out_scale) pushes the runtime (T,T) scale into a compile-time weight mul, and sdpa_fold_mul_nomask refolds. Opt-in: default_attention = DEFAULT + ATTENTION.

  • signals: law:sdpa_expand_s, law:naturality_scalar_left_rev, law:linear_out_scale
  • baselines: eager spelled (self) 32,772; inductor (compile) — measured-only (no symbolic form)

sdpa_nomask — No-mask spelled attention → fused SDPA ​

softmax(q@kᵀ/√d) @ v with no mask — nn.MultiheadAttention's default path, and the spelling encoder stacks (BERT-style bidirectional attention) export verbatim. The add/masked_fill fold family cannot see it; sdpa_fold_div_nomask is its row — minted by fold_object + guarded-compose and promoted after the sweep (project/retros/promoted-laws.md). The fold removes the materialised (T,T) score matrix, same transform as the masked row.

  • signals: law:sdpa_fold_div_nomask
  • baselines: eager spelled (self) 33,415; F.scaled_dot_product_attention (ref) 32,772 — the fused kernel, explicit scale=1/√d; inductor (compile) — measured-only (no symbolic form)

select_mul — select(a)⊙select(b) → select(a⊙b) commutation ​

sel(u) ⊙ sel(v) = sel(u ⊙ v) — the elementwise product commutes with the select view (select_mul, census-minted on DeltaRuleBlock-class slices; project/retros/select-mul-broadcast.md; the first machine-discovered law admitted, human-integrated). Honest row: standalone, the fused form pays for the product over the full tensor then slices — costlier than multiplying two slices — so the fold's win needs shared-slice consumers. With the search priced on the case's own launch axis the law still fires (the derivation is the signal) but extraction correctly DECLINES the fused member — the old +153 % row judged an exec-model pick on an axis it was never asked to optimize.

  • signals: law:select_mul
  • baselines: eager spelled (self) 15; mul-then-select (ref) 38; inductor (compile) — measured-only (no symbolic form)

silu_fold — x·σ(x) spelled by hand → fused silu kernel ​

SiLU (Elfwing et al. 2018; Ramachandran et al. 2017 "swish") spelled x·σ(x) folds to the one kernel — silu_fold, the definitional inverse of silu_expand and the mediating 3-cell of the silu_expand × swiglu_fuse critical pair (project/retros/law-coherence-catalogue.md §4). Inductor's pointwise-pattern tables carry the same identity (mul(x, sigmoid(x)) → silu).

  • signals: law:silu_fold
  • baselines: eager spelled (self) 194; F.silu (ref) 193 — the fused kernel; inductor (compile) — measured-only (no symbolic form)

softmax_fold — exp/Σexp manual softmax → fused softmax kernel ​

exp(u) / Σ exp(u) is softmax by definition — the composed-then- reduced chain the law pipeline's pattern-recognition pass recognised in the model census (the second machine-discovered law admitted; project/retros/law-proposer-extensions.md, project/retros/law-softmax-fold-shipped.md). Every graph compiler performs this fold (XLA's softmax rewriter, TFLite's IdentifySoftmax); here it lands as one dispatched op with a certificate.

  • signals: law:softmax_fold
  • baselines: eager spelled (self) 327; torch.softmax (ref) 65 — the fused kernel; inductor (compile) — measured-only (no symbolic form)

softsign_fold — x/(|x|+1) spelled by hand → fused softsign kernel ​

x / (|x| + 1) is softsign (Bergstra et al. 2009, the bounded rational activation) spelled in decomposed div/abs/add form — softsign_fold mints the one fused kernel, discovered by fold_object and promoted after the guarded sweep measured the region unguarded-clean (project/retros/promoted-laws.md).

  • signals: law:softsign_fold
  • baselines: eager spelled (self) 387; F.softsign (ref) 387 — the fused kernel; inductor (compile) — measured-only (no symbolic form)

swiglu_mlp — Two-projection SwiGLU → widened-GEMM fuse ​

LLaMA's SwiGLU MLP (Shazeer 2020, GLU variants): silu(x@A.T) ⊙ (x@B.T) fuses to one (2d,d) projection plus a split — the pairing pass (paired_extract) or swiglu_fuse. Measured 2.00-2.11x vs torch.compile on the delivered artifact (docs/results.md).

  • signals: stat:paired_extract
  • baselines: eager two-GEMM (self) 4,356; fused SwiGLU (ref) 4,485 — one widened Linear + split — the hand-fused impl; inductor (compile) — measured-only (no symbolic form)

weight_chain_fold — Two-layer Linear chain → weights-first fold ​

linear(linear(x,A),B) → linear(x, B·A) — assoc_linear reaches the weights-first form whose param-side matmul folds at compile time. The reassoc_scale result (docs/results.md): the e-graph finds a form Inductor's post-grad graph cannot express (no weight folding without freezing).

  • signals: law:assoc_linear
  • baselines: eager two-GEMM (self) 4,098; pre-folded Linear (ref) 2,049 — one Linear carrying W2@W1 — what weight folding ships; inductor (compile) — no freeze — Inductor cannot fold the weights

weight_merge — linear(x,A)+linear(x,B) → linear(x, A+B) ​

The LoRA-apply / model-soup merge deployment tooling performs by hand: two parallel projections of the same input sum into one GEMM on the summed weight — weight_factor_linear (transpose distributes over +). The parameter add is compile-time work, so runtime drops a GEMM: the bias-free analogue of the biased chain's assoc_linear_bias.

  • signals: law:weight_factor_linear
  • baselines: eager two-GEMM (self) 4,163; merged Linear (ref) 2,049 — one Linear carrying A+B — the merged form; inductor (compile) — measured-only (no symbolic form)

zoo_adaln_affd — Zoo AdaLNBlock — the affd carrier lift, opt-in regime ​

The held-out zoo's AdaLNBlock (Peebles & Xie 2022, DiT) — LN(x)·(1+scale) + shift modulation lifted by affd_lift / affd_lift_unit into applyd(aff_diag(ln(x), shift), 1+scale) under the carrier_search regime (SCAN_DIAG_LAWS is opt-in — DEFAULT never runs it; the store's affd_step_lift twin is admitted but unshipped). The lift lands AND pays: −8.1 % on the exec axis, below the eager floor. The former +7.9 % was a selection artifact, not the lift's price — _carrier_upgrade moved extraction to a fully-lifted member whose single-leaf "batched" plan priced below generic in the executor model, though a 0-level plan is pure gather/stack machinery (the human-bar.md regression). build_scan_plan now declines spines with no compose levels — nothing to level-schedule — so the genuinely cheaper lifted member stands: the same verdict the store arm's −4.07 % hinted at, now reached through the shipped opt-in laws.

  • signals: law:affd_lift, op:applyd
  • baselines: eager (self) 217,830; inductor (compile) — measured-only (no symbolic form)

zoo_cosine_attention — Zoo CosineAttention — om_lift on the normalized-score map ​

The held-out zoo's CosineAttention (SwinV2-style) — softmax(τ·q̂k̂ᵀ) with L2-normalized projections spelled linalg_vector_norm. om_lift (the carrier OM_LIFT, metavar-dim guard) raises the normalized outer-product map onto the om_apply/om_elem carrier. Replays the −0.006 % human-bar.md cell; honest on both axes — the executor reads −0.007 %, the launch axis reads a +2.9 % regression the scorecard records as beat=no.

  • signals: law:om_lift, op:om_apply
  • baselines: eager spelled (self) 348,463; inductor (compile) — measured-only (no symbolic form)

zoo_delta_rule — Zoo DeltaRuleBlock — partial aff-carrier lift on the write ​

The held-out zoo's DeltaRuleBlock (Schlag et al. 2021, DeltaNet) — the unrolled matrix-state S ← S + β·k·(v − kᵀS) write the census minted mul_unsq_nat from. The full chunked delta rule is honestly NOT in the library (the zoo's own docstring says so), but the diagonal-affine monoid still lifts the per-step write: applyd / aff_diag pairs absorb all fifteen spelled adds while mul_unsq_nat and select_mul fire on their source op-tuples — a partial carrier find measured −15.7 % launch / −16.1 % exec where the retro table recorded — under its pipeline axis.

  • signals: law:decay_state_lift_post, law:mul_unsq_nat, law:select_mul, op:applyd
  • baselines: eager unrolled (self) 975,073; inductor (compile) — measured-only (no symbolic form); FLA chunk_delta_rule (external) unavailable — Triton WY-repr kernel — scratch dep; the impl the full rule targets

zoo_gau — Zoo GatedAttentionUnit — quadratic gate lifts to om_decayed ​

The held-out zoo's GatedAttentionUnit (Hua et al. 2022, FLASH) — softmax-free attention whose relu(u uᵀ)² self-similarity score is a quadratic (T,T) site. om_decayed_lift raises the masked-score matmul onto the decayed-om carrier while the mul_square / square_to_pow spelling bridge canonicalises a*a; the extraction keeps one om_decayed node (the (T,T) score matrix never materialises). Replays the −0.003 % human-bar.md cell — −9.5 % under the executor axis here.

  • signals: law:om_decayed_lift, law:mul_square, op:om_decayed
  • baselines: eager quadratic (self) 182,973; inductor (compile) — measured-only (no symbolic form); FLASH GAU kernel (external) unavailable — fla.layers.gau — scratch dep; the hand kernel this emulates

zoo_lora_adapter — Zoo LoRAAdapter — the assoc_linear win, replayed ​

The held-out zoo's own LoRAAdapter (Hu et al. 2021): the real W x + alpha·(B (A x)) production spelling, imported verbatim from mlopt_discovery.zoo — not a purpose-built respelling. assoc_linear folds the low-rank chain up(down(x)) into one dense GEMM whose B·A weight product folds at compile time.
