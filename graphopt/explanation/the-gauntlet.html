The gauntlet — what a minted law must survive ​

A candidate law is proposed cheaply and admitted expensively. The admission gauntlet is the pipeline every minted law runs before it may enter DEFAULT — and the reason the shipped bank can be trusted without trusting the proposer.

text
propose → numeric oracle → derivability check → typed-pay gate
        → adversarial sweep → certificate replay → MACHINE_SHIPPED

The stages ​

  1. Numeric oracle. Both sides of the candidate equality execute on real tensors (seeded fixtures, fp64) and must agree — equality is tested, not assumed.
  2. Derivability check. verify_law(lhs, rhs, rules) puts both sides in a fresh small e-graph and saturates under the known laws — the e-graph is the decision procedure for e-class membership. On a merge it emits a positional certificate and replays it on real terms; the certificate is tried in both directions so a witness is a genuine derivation, never a trusted stub.
  3. The typed-pay gate. The candidate must actually pay: measured wall/peak cost on the fixtures, under the three-costs discipline — dag_cost ranking is never enough.
  4. The adversarial sweep. mlopt_discovery.gap_gen generates bindings the corpus cannot — witnesses inside the guard's false region — and tries to falsify the candidate's side conditions. This is where the teeth are: the sweep killed 212 bad accepts on mul_unsq_nat's first (plain broadcast-equality) guard, caught four false twins, and found a real lowering bug no test covered.
  5. Certificate + provenance. The survivor ships in DEFAULT with MACHINE_SHIPPED provenance — replayable at import, auditable via law_provenance(rule).

The worked example — mul_unsq_nat ​

The first law minted from a corpus op-tuple rather than a hand-declared need. The census saw mul(unsqueeze, unsqueeze) in a real model's β_t·k_t write; the candidate mul(unsqueeze U d, unsqueeze V d) = unsqueeze(mul U V, d) was proposed; auto_cond's first guard was falsified by the sweep (212 bad accepts); the exact flat-map-unsq guard was synthesized instead — 0 bad accepts, 0 misses; it pays −12.27% on its source workload and ships in DEFAULT. Its apparent sibling (mul_unsqueeze_r_id) was correctly rejected — its real sites are semantically unequal, and the guard declines all of them.

The referee contract ​

The gauntlet emits a plain {axis: value} measurement; the ScoringSpec on the game side turns it into a verdict — score(spec, measured) returns gates cleared, the weighted value, and the axes the measurement lacked. The two sides meet only through that mapping: measurement code never imports the scoring module, and the scoring module imports no measurement machinery.

The honest boundary ​

Admission is regime-bounded: fixtures are small, seeded, one hardware box. The gauntlet's job is to make the claim falsifiable and bounded, not universal — a law that pays on its fixtures and replays its certificate ships; the scope of "pays" is part of the record.
