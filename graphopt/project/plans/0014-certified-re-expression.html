Plan 0014 — Certified re-expression: from proofs to delivery ​

Goal ​

Convert the categorical reach the engine already proves into delivered performance and memory where structure exists. Today the project is theory-rich and conversion-poor: it finds the structures (carriers, scans, streaming softmax, KV sharing) but the carrier forms lose to Inductor's fused kernels (per-leaf dispatch).

The plan makes architecture the variable — search certified structural variants (head/KV sharing, expert tying, adapter merging, residual reassociation) and deliver the cheapest variant within a proven error_budget.

Delivery — two paths ​

Delivery is not one problem; it splits by whether the extracted program expresses in Inductor's op set:

  • Standard forms (fold, reassociation, pairing, KV share, head collapse, tying) are ordinary matmul/add/etc. Hand Inductor the better program and it fuses and schedules it — the win lands, no gap to close. Extraction already routes here: backend_cost(cost_fn, sink.supported_ops) only prices forms the backend can lower.
  • Carrier forms (scan, streaming softmax, batched levels) are outside Inductor's reach, so each leaf dispatches separately and the carrier loses. These need the fused executor (stage 1).

Most architecture-level wins are the first kind; the fused executor is a carrier-only concern.

Mechanism — structural interpretation ​

The model graph is syntax (ops = generators of the free monoidal category, wires = objects). A structural interpretation is a functor from that free category into a category of structures, and the structure supplies the laws: monoid → associativity (Blelloch), comonoid → Δ (pairing), traced → feedback (resolvents), hypergraph → contraction order. The interpretation adds no information — the function is fixed — it adds legality: which transformations are valid.

The interpretation is not unique. The same graph reads as plain composition (nothing to do), shared-input comonoid (pairing), low-rank (bounded), or a contraction problem — each exposing different rewrites. So discovery = choosing the richest interpretation, not applying laws inside a fixed one. That is what carrier recognition, the morphism engine, and 0013 do at successive levels.

Reach is the closure of the generators, not the generators themselves: the law library is the generating 2-cells, and the free higher category they generate is the reachable set. The e-graph already computes this higher structure (1-cells programs, 2-cells rewrites, 3-cells coherence via _close_congruence); budgets truncate it, and a truncation can miss but never mis-prove.

Stages ​

  1. Real-block lifting (the gate). _sig_liftable declines multi-activation and mutated-cache blocks, so a real KV-cache attention block (x, kv_cache) never enters the search. Classify the cache as read-only state and lift multi-input attention blocks. Nothing downstream moves until this lands.

  2. Fused carrier executor (carrier-only). Measured carrier losses are per-leaf dispatch, not search. Fuse/tile scan_lower / om_lower, or capture decode into a CUDA graph, so a proven carrier converts to wall-time and memory instead of losing to Inductor's fused kernels. Standard-op wins need none of this — they ride Inductor (see Delivery).

  3. Certified variant search (architecture as the variable). Extend the morphism engine from match-and-apply to a search over structural variants, priced by a diagram-level cost model, each accepted only under a certificate (exact or within error_budget). Targets the bounded wins that already reproduce (tied-head collapse, 1.15–1.32×) and the named niche (decode KV, adapters, redundant heads).

  4. Structure zoo + production scale (the validation). A corpus of structured models (GQA, MoE, LoRA-merged, MLA-style KV, tied heads) and measurement at production dims (d≥1024, B≥8, real checkpoint, datacenter GPU). Required for any paper claim; the (now-retired) research writeup listed it as blocking.

  5. Contraction search (0013) — the discovery feeder. Replace the hand-written morphism laws with contraction-order search so sharing is found, not hand-coded. Sequenced after delivery works: discovering structure you cannot deliver is a proof, not a speedup.

Bar ​

  • Delivery: the fused carrier path beats Inductor on decode latency / peak memory in the structured regime — not raw prefill GEMM throughput.
  • Conversion: a reified structural win converts to wall-time or memory at a rate that makes search worth its compile cost.
  • Scale: ≥1.2× on a real checkpoint at production dims with certificates, or a documented reason why not.

Risks ​

  • Carrier delivery gap is a different discipline. Codegen/fusion for carriers is not categorical rewriting; closing it may need work outside the thesis. Standard-op wins ride Inductor and are unaffected — but a carrier win without a competitive executor is a proof, not a speedup.
  • Structure is conditional. The census says stock dense models are thin; the wins live in structured models, so the zoo and the honest scope are load-bearing.
  • Diagram cost model. Search is blind without a cost that ranks variants before reify; measure rank correlation (as cost_fidelity.py does) before trusting the search.
  • Compile time. 262s at T=2048 vs Inductor's 45s — construction over saturation is also a compile-time lever.

Non-goals ​

  • Weight-space compression (ADR 0001).
  • Beating Inductor on raw prefill GEMM throughput.
  • Reinforcement learning. Directed search (bound + decompose + beam) handles the exponential joint move space — the Catalan bracketing search already does — because structure is what makes search beat RL.
