Plan 0024 — the form-level proposer and vocabulary minting ​

Depends on: ADR 0005 (the open game), plan 0023 (the carrier), catopt_discovery.enumerate (the instance enumerator).

Motivation ​

The enumerator closes the instance half of candidate generation: 27 rewriting forms (comm/assoc/factor/pull/hom/…) instantiated over the op grammar, deduped and classified against the library — 1,160 candidates at depth ≤2, five paying laws auto-found. That is enumeration within a fixed form list.

Two surfaces remain human:

  1. The form list itself. _f_comm, _f_factor_left, … are hand-written skeletons. A shape the list lacks is unreachable — the same wall the fold hit, where the machine needed the K-way construct and a human had to design it.
  2. What to mint. The minting machinery is built (composite ops, declared atoms + generated kernels, handlers, the cell store), but which atom to propose is human-supplied. The game can mint anything expressible; nothing proposes it.

Both are the same question one level up: propose the shape, not the instance.

What exists (don't rebuild) ​

  • enumerate.py — the 27 forms + _form_pairs round-robin, _filter_reason pre-filters, alpha_key dedup, _relation classification.
  • proposal.evaluate — numeric oracle, cost delta, verdicts; rank_pool — learned/heuristic/random AP.
  • auto_cond_object — synthesizes the guard a candidate needs from its measured domain (the game minted xy+xz=x(y+z) iff x≡y).
  • dspec_synth — synthesizes a declarative derive from a hook's measured outputs; refusals name the missing bank op.
  • object_synthesis — compose, specialize, relax_guard, _mint_guarded; cellstore.admit_object + run_gauntlet.
  • open_game — the enumerated stream as a playable board with a trained prior.

The construct ​

A. Form-level enumeration ​

Generate skeletons, not instances. A form is a small meta-term: op-classes (binary/unary/literal) at nodes and metavariables at leaves, with the lhs/rhs pair constrained to a single algebraic identity. The 27 shipped forms are a hand sample of this space; the space itself is generatable.

  1. enumerate_forms(*, max_nodes, arities, classes) — a lazy stream of skeleton pairs over a meta-grammar (node = op-class + children; leaf = metavar or literal-class), with the shipped forms recoverable as members (a test asserts each _f_* skeleton appears in the stream — the stream is a superset, so nothing regresses).
  2. Sound-by-construction filters only (arity/class match, tautology, uninstantiable metavar); everything else flows to the existing evaluate + gauntlet — the refusal machinery is already honest, so the form level inherits it.
  3. The open game's board generalizes: a move may be a form (whose instances are then evaluated) or an instance — the prior learns which forms pay, which is the transferable knowledge the instance-level prior cannot express.

B. Vocabulary minting ​

Propose an atom when a measured need names one:

  1. Need detection. Two existing signals: (a) dspec_synth's refusals (_near_misses naming the missing bank op — pow today); (b) a candidate class that repeatedly fails only on an expressibility gap (the same subterm shape recurring in saturated graphs across models with no law covering it — minable from the census).
  2. Proposal. Emit a declare move for a declared op + a generated kernel (the handler path that already exists), with the semantics verified observationally against the spelled form on real tensors — the ADR-0005 floor (substrate-expressibility, not the shipping op list).
  3. Admission. Unchanged: numeric oracle → typed pay gate → certificate replay → adversarial sweep. A minted atom that does not pay in the cost model is admitted as vocabulary but not preferred — the honest outcome.

Measured outcome (2026-10-15) — part A ran ​

catopt_discovery.forms is built (committed b0c3e2d): lazy skeleton enumeration over the meta-grammar, candidate instantiation reusing the existing filters/dedup, and the falsification harness. All 28 shipped skeletons are recoverable (superset verified).

The falsification is NOT triggered — but the reading is narrow. Over 12,000 candidates (default 8-op vocab, max_nodes=3) the machine admitted 7 paying laws from forms outside the shipped 27 — the cross-op-agreement family (sub(X,0)=add(X,0), div(X,1)=mul(X,1), B0(X,K)=B1(K,X), …). Each is a composite re-spelling of two shipped identities, so the new algebraic content is thin.

Honest conclusion: the form list is not the whole grammar, but the forms it lacks are shallow. The form level is a real capability (it reaches shapes a human list omitted) and it does not, by itself, produce a deep discovery. The open question moves to part B — whether a minted atom (vocabulary, not a re-spelling) can pay where no composition does.

Stream sizes: small vocab (2 bin/2 un) = 7,252,000 skeletons at max_nodes=3; the 8-op default is effectively unbounded — the stream is lazy and capped by max_forms/max_candidates.

pow — the gap this plan named — unblocks no shipped carrier rule; its refusal was the synthetic probe's, not a real derive's. The residual's actual need is % axis normalisation (mod). And the ~29 carrier-aware planner declines (_cat_axis_plan / _key_cat_plan) are unblocked by no arity-2 op — they need a carrier-aware shape source, i.e. a structural gap, not vocabulary.

Conclusion for part B: need detection works and is honest (proposal ≠ admission; admitted: False always), and its value is the measured ordering — the plan's guess about which op was missing was wrong, and the machinery said so. The remaining surface is structural (shape sources), which is the next level up again.

Measured outcome (2026-10-15) — the shape source is a zero ​

The structural gap was addressed (committed 9e7b462): a ("vshape", T) op in both DSL dispatch tables plus a register_value_shape sink seam (core cannot import carriers), with xcarrier registering _xshape.

Rules unblocked: 0 — and the reason is the finding. The synthesis measurement domain (oracle._binding_envs via _measure_domain) binds leaf terms only, so vshape_diff_envs == 0 on all 27 declines and _xshape ≡ _shape_of there. The value-shape gap is real only at law-evaluation time, where any_term resolves a metavar to a carrier member — a domain the synth cannot reach.

So plan 0024's diagnosis was half-right: the shape source was necessary but not sufficient. What actually blocks the remaining declines is the synth's expression/guard bank (if/guard/%/dim composition) and multi-output fitting — not the shape source. Eleven of the 27 are constant domains (no signal at all).

Revised next target: deepen the measurement domain (bind op-terms, not just leaves) so a carrier-aware derive is measurable, and extend the expression bank with guarded composition. The DSL capability is now in place and verified (_shape_of == (4,) vs _xshape == (5,) on apply(aff(A,c),h)).

Falsification ​

  • Form level: over a fixed evaluation budget, does form-level enumeration admit laws from forms outside the shipped 27 at a useful rate? If every admission is a re-instantiation of a shipped form, the level adds nothing and the honest conclusion is that the form list is the real grammar.
  • Vocabulary: does need-detection propose an atom that admits and pays, without a human naming it? The pow gap is the first candidate — the refusal is already measured.

Metrics to report (not assert) ​

  • yield per oracle eval: form-level vs instance-level;
  • admitted laws attributable to a new form (the discovery count, which must be >0 for the claim);
  • vocabulary proposals: proposed / admitted / paid;
  • the honest negative: what still needs a human and why (the refusal reasons, enumerated).

Scars to respect ​

  • No comm/assoc in default saturation (measured OOM at bounded budgets on the 11 GB host).
  • Closure-generating constructs stay opt-in and budgeted (OM_CHUNKIFY, om_kway_laws, POINTWISE_FACTOR_RULES).
  • dspec_synth refuses on thin/degenerate sample sets — the form-level proposer inherits that discipline (held-out validation, no coincidence fits).
  • The measured win is the gate: an admission that does not pay is data, not a result.
