Plan 0023 — the sequential carrier (state-level fold) ​

Depends on 0005 (the open game, phase 1 — the measured win).

Context ​

Every piece of the delivery loop works: machine-derived applyd/aff_diag carriers mint handlers, generate verified Triton kernels, get measured and costed. The single gap to the O(T·C) claim is producing the sequential carrier member — the e-graph never spells the cross-block contribution as a state-level fold.

Diagnosis (verified 2026-10, probe scripts in session logs) ​

On the real ChunkedRetention export, after OM_LAWS + OM_CHUNKIFY + SPLIT_SCALAR_RULES saturation (14 iters, 40/40 chunk budgets):

  • applyd enodes exist — depth 1 only, zero affd_compose chains. The fold machinery chains correctly in isolation (depth-2 on ((w8·c0) + (w4·c1)) + c2), so the lifts aren't the blocker.
  • mul(·, applyd) members: 0 — affd_lift_step's lhs (add(mul(a, applyd), x)) has nothing to bind.
  • matmul(·, add(mul(…),·)) members: 0 — the state-level weighted sum never forms either.
  • Why: contributions arrive as matmul(mul(score_slice, decay_slice), v_slice) — the decay is an elementwise mask inside the matmul's left operand, not a scalar on a folded state.
  • om_decayed_cross never fires at the factored sites: decay_block_cross produces decay as a deeper unsqueeze-product chain (all factors unsqueeze(·, dim=-1), a multi-factor product), not the strict two-factor mul(unsq(ξ), unsq(η)) the law's lhs requires. And _vshape returns None at the split members, so even if the lhs matched, the check can't verify the broadcasts.

So the chain om_decayed_cross → matmul_col_decay → assoc_matmul → q @ (γ^C·S + c) stops at step 1.

The construct (ordered candidates) ​

  1. Generalized cross law — om_decayed(s, mul(X, unsq(η,UE)), v) → mul(X, matmul(mul(s, unsq(η,UE)), v)): X absorbs the residual row-side factors (any row-broadcaster, not just unsq(ξ)). Subsumes the current strict law. Guarded: X's broadcast shape must cover the row axis, the unsq the column axis, and the inner matmul must type. (Watch the unsqueeze-dim polarity — the observed chain has every factor at dim=-1; which factor plays the η role needs confirming by shape, not position.)
  2. Decay normalization pass — a small law that rewrites the n-factor decay product into the canonical mul(unsq(ξ), unsq(η)) form first (reassociate + merge same-axis factors), then the existing om_decayed_cross applies. More laws but no new semantics.
  3. K-way chunk law — om_decayed → K-block split (sizes=(C,)*K) so row blocks see ≥2 prior contributions; pairwise bisection structurally caps the fold at depth 1 (one prior block per split). Complements 1/2 — the bridge still has to produce the foldable spelling.

Progress (2026-10-13) ​

  • OM_DECAYED_CROSS_GEN landed — the generalized variant (x absorbs residual row-factors; guarded by broadcast-constancy on the contracted axis). Fires 4× on the real export; the ξηᵀ split now happens (matmul(mul(s,unsq(eta)),v) members exist).
  • Stale diagnosis corrected: the strict om_decayed_cross ALSO fires in the baseline (e-class matching sees the lone unsq inside the deeper-product class), and _vshape resolves split members fine — neither was the true blocker.
  • Next link (exact): the produced members are matmul(mul(s, unsq(eta)), v) — decay inside the matmul's left operand. matmul_col_decay wants mul(matmul(q,kT), unsq(eta)) — decay outside. The bridge now needed: either matmul(mul(s,e),v) → mul(?, matmul(s, mul(eta,k))-shaped laws pushing the factor through, or the state-level fold directly.
  • mul(·,applyd) and matmul(·,add(mul)) still 0; applyd depth still 1.
  • Div-naturality landed: mul_div_swap ((a/b)·c=(a·c)/b, unguarded) + matmul_div_distribute ((a/c)@v=(a@v)/c, scalar c) — 48 div-over-matmul members produced; mul(mm,eta) now reaches matmul_col_decay's lhs and its check PASSES (bindings resolve via any_term; verified 24 matmul(q,transpose/mul) members). Remaining: the outer add of weighted state contributions inside the matmul's right operand — the accumulator itself, not the inner decay.
  • Phase-2 side: catopt_discovery.enumerate — 1,160 candidates at depth≤2, 5 useful-new paying laws auto-found, FALSE controls verified.

Progress (2026-10-14) — the state accumulator lands ​

  • matmul(·, add(·,·)) member produced on the real export (T=32, 16 iters, 60/60 chunk budgets, ~3 s): matmul(q_i, add(S'_0, S'_1)) — the inter-chunk weighted state INSIDE the contraction, numerically verified against the class value (2.2e-15). The rhs add also carries applyd and mul(γ,·) members — the folded spelling.
  • The chain that lands it, all shipped laws:
    1. SPLIT_FLATTEN — nested same-axis splits collapse to flat partitions ((16,8,8), (8,8,16)), unblocking deeper sites.
    2. DECAY_BLOCK_CROSS_FLAT — the strict-lower block law under MIXED partitions (range test sum(SZR[:I]) ≥ sum(SZC[:J+1]), subsumes the J < I sibling): deeper decay_vec factorizations at split(split(decay,(16,8,8),-2,I),(16,16), -1,J) sites — 9 decay_vec enodes, up from 5.
    3. DECAY_STATE_WEIGHT_SINK — (a⊙b)⊙(q@B) → b⊙(q@(a⊙B)), the per-block γ^o weight detaches from the row vector γ^r and lands inside the state.
    4. DIV_ADD_FACTOR (core SIMPLIFICATION_RULES) — x/c + y/c = (x+y)/c for scalar c: the score √d exits the block sum so assoc_matmul_rev + factor_matmul can collect the shared q_i under one right-operand add.
    5. DECAY_STATE_WEIGHT_EXIT — q@(a⊙B) → a⊙(q@B) (inverse twin of matmul_scalar_factor): mul(γ^o, S) addends — decay_state_lift's lhs — appear inside the operand.
  • A real unsoundness found and fixed: matmul_scalar_factor's guard passed a (T,1) row-decay vector into the contraction — c[r]⊙(q@B) ≠ q@(c[k]⊙B), measured diff ≈16.36. Guard now requires contraction-axis constancy (right-aligned c[-2] == 1) via _const_along_contraction, shared by the sink/exit checks.
  • Saturated counts (16-iter probe): ~620 classes, applyd 63, matmul(·,add) 1; extraction of the saturated root verifies against the module's output (5e-8 fp64 carrier-eval tolerance).
  • Still not done for the plan's acceptance: extraction preferring the fold under the carrier cost model on a T=512 probe, and profitable measuring the minted kernel — those run on the delivery side, not the e-graph side.

Progress (2026-10-13, evening) ​

  • Reachability: DONE. On the real ChunkedRetention export the saturated e-graph contains the sequential carrier — matmul(q_i, add(S'₀, S'₁)) with applyd/mul(γ,·) members in the add class that decay_state_lift folds. Value-consistent (2.2e-15); root still equals the module output (5e-8 fp64).
  • Five-law chain: split_flatten, decay_block_cross_flat, decay_state_weight_sink, div_add_factor, decay_state_weight_exit — all guarded, all sweep-clean.
  • Caught a real bug: matmul_scalar_factor was unsound on (T,1) row vectors (measured diff 16.36); guard now requires contraction-axis constancy.
  • Preference: DONE (corrected). Under the full reachability law set (+ MUL_DIV_SWAP, MATMUL_DIV_DISTRIBUTE, ASSOC_MATMUL_REV, FACTOR_MUL, FACTOR_MUL_ADD, DIV_ADD_FACTOR, 16 iters, 60/60) extraction at T=512 picks the chunked cross-block carrier: 29,386,752 vs 39,059,456 FLOPs (27,618,304 at 120/120). The earlier "fused still wins" reading used the limited set — the naturality laws are load-bearing. Pinned by test_extraction_prefers_the_chunked_carrier_at_t512.
  • Measured end-to-end: ChunkedRetention gen_probe 866–1113 µs vs compiled 2018–2028 µs → 1.82–2.33×, fp64 max_abs ≈ 1.3e-8. Scope: the paying claims are the decayed fused kernel + an elementwise gen kernel; the scan kernels are correctly dropped as unloweable.
  • Robustness fix: profitable aborted the sweep when a harvested handler's kernel raised (unloweable depth-1 sites, 512 vs 16 broadcast) — now declines; ChunkedRetention measures on CUDA again.
  • Tried and reverted: a stricter _scan_sites broadcast filter (drop aff_diag sites whose a/b/h can't broadcast). Measured no-op — the state operand's shape resolves as a non-tuple, so the guard passes it through; the motivating sites (512 vs 16) still harvest. The graceful decline in profitable is the correct and sufficient fix; a real filter needs a shape resolver that decodes carrier states, which is a bigger piece.
  • Corrected (2026-10-15, measured): the gap is a SEARCH gap, not pricing. A fold is a cost-neutral relabelling — flops_cost(applyd(aff_diag(a,x),h)) == flops_cost(add(mul(a,h),x)) exactly; _applyd_flops prices O(state·steps) = O(T·C), the same as the spelled sum. Extraction is therefore right to keep the spelled concat: every fold-containing root member costs ≥ it (T=512,k=2: extract 6,700,800 vs cheapest fold member 11,340,800; k=8: 8,572,928 at depth 1, 22,980,244 at depth 7). basic.py untouched — no fix warranted.
  • The deep fold does exist as a finite root member (honest metric: 3 at k=2, 7 = T/C−1 at k=8) — it is a tree, not a shared recurrence. (The earlier depth readings came from a cycle-inflated probe metric; the buildability least-fixpoint is the correct one.)
  • The missing construct: reassociate the cross sum Σ_{j<i} γ^{(i-j)C} b_j into the nested shared form b_{i-1} + γ^C(b_{i-2} + γ^C(…)), so the state is shared across row blocks and the existing decay_state_lift chain folds it. A hand-built shared-state fold prices 589,952 vs the extracted 6,700,800 — 11.4× — so the O(T·C) win is real and unreached. The pieces exist (matmul_col_decay, decay_vec_offset, matmul_scalar_factor, decay_state_weight_sink/exit); the reassociation that makes the sum shared is absent. It needs a soundness guard — do not invent it unguarded.

Progress (2026-10-15, late) — the reassociation law shipped ​

om_shared_state_laws() (committed 809b50e): three opt-in laws reassociating the weighted cross sum into the nested shared form, guarded (aligned integer offsets, shared decay base/axis, _const_along_contraction for the mat variant) and fp64-exact (~2.2e-16) for scalar and per-position weights.

  • The mechanism works: a controlled 2-block test shows the reassociated state as one e-class with 2 parents, DAG cost 172 → 116. On the real export: 10.8–24.5% (T=512, K=8: 8,572,928 → 7,643,912).
  • Acceptance NOT met — blocker exact: sharing needs an i-independent state (the key-value block B_j), but the export's score is q(x)@k(x)ᵀ/√t, so the graph only reaches div(matmul(q_i, B_j), c) — the scalar division wraps the contraction and om_shared_mat_reassoc fires 0× at K=8.
  • New finding — a rule-set conflict: the natural bridge (div(matmul(A,B),c) → matmul(div(A,c),B), exact for scalar c) loops with the shipped MATMUL_DIV_DISTRIBUTE. The two cannot coexist unbudgeted; the fix is scheduling (mutually exclusive rule sets / staged saturation) or a div-tolerant variant (fires 126× at T=32 but saves marginally).
  • So the remaining gap is search-reachability + rule scheduling, not algebra. The 11.4× prize is still unreached.

Progress (2026-10-15) — construct 3: the K-way chunk law lands ​

om_decayed_kway_blocks(k) (packages/catopt-carriers/src/catopt_carriers/om.py, opt-in via om_kway_laws(k)): om_decayed(s, decay(g,T), v) → concat_i(intra_i + Σ_{j<i} cross_ij) for the uniform (C,)*K partition (C = T/K) — diagonal blocks regenerate decay(g,C), strict-lower blocks factor as the rank-1 ξ_i·η_jᵀ outer product. ONE application reaches K chunks; guarded by the usual dim/rank checks, numerically verified fp64 (≤9e-16), and swept clean. It is a closure generator (its diagonal blocks re-match the lhs) so it stays out of OM_LAWS, budgeted like OM_CHUNKIFY.

Progress (2026-10-16) — the fold's depth is NOT the blocker; the fold's cost is ​

Re-ran the probe and decoded the root class's members (lab/probes/om_decayed_kway_probe.py, plus a member/decode harness). Measured (T=512 unless noted, seeded, OM_LAWS + OM_CHUNKIFY + SPLIT_SCALAR_RULES + the 6 bridges + om_kway_laws(k)):

  • The deep fold EXISTS as a finite term. A cycle-safe finite fold-depth metric (buildability = least fixpoint, then level-by-level fold-headed reachability) gives T/C-1 exactly at k=8 (depth 7) and 3 at k=2 — the acceptance depth is reached. The probe's own DFS metric is cycle-inflated on the k=2/4 rows (it can walk an applyd→applyd e-class cycle no finite term realises); the finite depth agrees at k=8 and is the honest number.
  • The root class has no applyd-headed member. Its members are concat/div/add/matmul/om_decayed only. affd_compose enodes: 0 in the everyday set (the compose-step lift lives in SCAN_DIAG_LAWS, not OM_LAWS; adding it makes 234 affd_compose enodes yet extraction is unchanged).
  • The fold members that DO exist cost ≥ the spelled extraction. At k=8 the cheapest fold-containing root member is the sameconcat at the same cost (8,572,928 / 1,695,488 T=128); the deep (depth-7) member costs 2.7× more at T=512 (22.98M vs 8.57M). At k=2 the cheapest fold member is 11.34M vs the 6.70M extraction. So extraction is not mis-preferring the non-fold — the fold is never cheaper.
  • Why: applyd(aff_diag(a,x), h) prices EXACTLY like the add(mul(a,h),x) it re-spells (_applyd_flops = 2·numel(state); mul+add = the same). Folding is a cost-neutral relabelling; the e-graph's fold chain is a tree, not a shared recurrence, so it saves nothing. The applyd/aff_diag nodes themselves are negligible (7 × ~2048 FLOPs), so the cost model is NOT the blocker.
  • The paying fold is a SEARCH gap. A hand-built shared-state recurrence (S_i = applyd(aff_diag(γ^C, (η_i⊙k_i)ᵀv_i), S_{i-1}), each S_i one class consumed by block i's output and block i+1's step) prices 0.59M at K=32/C=16 — far below the 4.72M spelled cross and the 6.70M extraction. The missing move is the reassociation of the geometric-weighted cross sum Σ_{j<i} γ^{(i-j)C} b_j into the nested recurrence b_{i-1} + γ^C(b_{i-2} + γ^C(…)) so the existing decay_state_lift/affd_lift chain can fold it and the state is shared across row blocks. matmul_col_decay, decay_vec_offset, matmul_scalar_factor and the decay_state_weight_sink/exit bridges are already the right pieces; what is absent is the reassociation that makes the sum shared rather than a flat per-block tree. (Do not invent a new law for this without a soundness guard.)
