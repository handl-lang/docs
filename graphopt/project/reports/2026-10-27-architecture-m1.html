Architecture & layout — M1 (for review) ​

Form: lab (ADR-0159 not yet triggered). Repos, drivers and own harnesses; no ext wrapper, no FFI seam. Naming: opt-graph (today graphopt), opt-kernel (today kernelopt); the IR is ADR-0160.

Corrections from review (accepted) ​

1. lib-kernel-ir was the wrong noun. A kit is a curated manifest of handl libs; a lib is a package. So the vocabulary is lib-kernel-ir (a handl library: ops, grades, costs, denotations), and kit-kernel-ir (M2) is the curated kit that includes it alongside the base types. Two artefacts, two nouns, as every other kit/lib pair in the org.

2. compile-triton is Rust. Every engine in the compile-* family is Rust; emitting Triton is string generation over a term, so it belongs there too — deterministic, no Python runtime, part of the engine family. Executing the emitted source needs Python, but that is the runtime binding (lib-triton-qgemv) or an ext, never the compiler.

3. There is no new schema. The IR is Term. This is the correction that matters: handl already represents computation as data — Term::{Arr, Seq, Op, Handle, Loop, App} with a dimension/grade slot. A schedule does not need a parallel IR format; it needs ops the kit declares (tile_load, split, dot, …), carried in Term::Op with a grade row, and an engine that interprets them. So:

before (wrong)after (right)
new KernelIR schema in lib-kernel-irTerm values with kit-declared ops
IR → Triton lowering in a bespoke modulecompile-triton interprets Term
denotations in the kitdenotations in lib-kernel-ir tests + laws

What this buys: the body is transparent in the platform's own representation, so the certificate, the sink, the sieve and the cost model all apply unchanged — no second universe to keep in sync. Where a schedule cannot be expressed as Term, that is a finding about the calculus, and the platform's rule applies: it enters by data (an op), a desugaring, or a handler — never by a new combinator.

Two-stage schedule, stated once: our IR decides blocks and split; Triton decides pipelining, vectorization and layouts. Reports must say which stage chose what, or a win cannot be attributed.

M1 boundary ​

In: ADR · lib-kernel-ir with nine ops + denotations · kit-kernel-ir (M2) manifest · compile-triton for qgemv with 4 split_k variants · equality gate vs awq_gemm · search driver emitting a best kernel and a routing row.

Out: lib-triton-qgemv binding, M>1 tiling, FP8/GPTQ, compile cache, compile-cuda, cost-model ranking (bound only).

Gate: all four variants cosine ≥ 0.9999 vs awq_gemm; at least one within the hand kernel's 58.9 µs cold on down_proj.
