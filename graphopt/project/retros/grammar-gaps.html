Retro: grammar gaps — closing the measured refusals, and the pay wall ​

Context: the ADR-0007 layer-2 handler grammar (mlopt_discovery.handler_synth) mints a non-elementwise atom by proposing a descriptor from a structured template vocabulary and verifying it fp64 against the pre-mint spec. The composed loop (lab/mint/atom_synth_loop.py) runs need -> handler -> laws -> admission -> pay. Five refusals were measured; this round closes four and names the fifth precisely.

Changed code lives in packages/mlopt-discovery/src/mlopt_discovery/handler_synth.py plus its test file; lab/audit/grammar_probe.py is the census instrument. Nothing under mlopt-core changed.

The extensions, each verified through the unchanged fp64 gate:

  • reduce family — ("reduce", op, sub, axis) over sum/mean/prod/amax/amin on the spec's own dim attrs plus -1/-2.
  • identity sides — an empty fold a/b partition spells its side's identity (1.0 for a, 0.0 for b); with the scalar-operand lift (_as_tensor, commit 311fac1) these are live again, so a carry-less recurrence is spellable.
  • neutral carries — a fold may start from 0.0/1.0 (FOLD_CARRIES), so a spec with no explicit carry meta reaches its canonical form.
  • norm family — ("norm", op, lhs, red_op, rhs, axis, keepdim), a value combined with a reduction of (another) value; the composite the reduce family's whole-spec operand cannot spell.

The budget heuristics, confirmed not relaxed ​

The fold/fold_emit family keeps its >= 3 meta floor: the one/two-meta mints all come from the fold_emit seed (_canonical_emit_seeds), and relaxing the floor only explodes the space — cumsum(X)'s fold_emit column grows from 36 (the seed) to 147700 candidates, a two-meta spec's from 144 to >200000, every battery spec mints the same body at the same index, and the applyd-nest fold_zip mint regresses out of a 1500 budget.

The emit projection depth is capped at one non-accumulator op (_MAX_EMIT_OPS): the emitted-scan corpus emit is $acc and the next useful form is one op; a two-op projection is outside the cap.

Dead space ​

The old dead region (bare-float fold fillers) was already lifted. This round found and removed two more: a non-keepdim normalization cannot broadcast its denominator back, and the both-identity fold (a=1.0, b=0.0) has no step axis. Measured on the cumsum spec: 143 of the first 600 candidates raised, 141 of them non-keepdim norm bodies; after deriving keepdim off the spec (_spec_keepdim), gating the norm family to normalization-shaped specs, and dropping the both-identity forms, 0 raise. The residual raises on two-meta specs are shape-incompatible contract einsums — a domain-shape refusal, not dead space.

The pay wall ​

The driver admits every handler need (cumprod 3 laws, cumsum 8, logcumsumexp 1, scan_fold 2) but no atom pays (delta 0.0 on every corpus term):

  • scan_fold — fire 0. Its only law that connects the atom to the corpus site, sf.corpus_refold, is rejected-undeclarable (envs=164 eq=3 bad=1 real=1): "no declarable conjunction of <= 3 covers 35 equal / 42 bad sites". The two certified laws (sf.scale, sf.split_same) are algebraic identities with no real sites, so nothing fires. This is the stabilization gap.
  • cumsum — fire 1, delta 0. One certified law (cum.add_const, the cumsum(add(x, c)) shape) fires once on one corpus term, but its rewrite is cost-neutral: it does not lower the extracted FLOPs. cumsum is ambient (already bound), so minting it adds no extraction power either.

The stabilization gap, measured ​

The corpus SelectiveScan site is mul(cumprod(sigmoid(A)), cumsum(B / clamp_min(cumprod(sigmoid(A)), 1e-6))); the minted atom is the ideal unclamped recurrence fold_emit(sigmoid(A), B, 0.0, $acc, -2). Evaluating both in fp64 across a sweep of the gate magnitude:

The mint is exact while the cumulative gate stays above 1e-6 and diverges by O(1) the moment it underflows. The verification domain (randn A -> sigmoid ~ 0.5, gate ~ 1e-4) sits in the inactive region, so the mint passes; the real site's gate underflows, so the recognition law is false there.

Can the grammar express a stabilized fold? No — not as an affine recurrence. The clamp acts on the accumulator (the cumulative product), not on a fixed operand: the stabilized scan is a two-state, non-linear recurrence

P_t = clamp_min(sigmoid(A_t) * P_{t-1}, eps)
S_t = S_{t-1} + B_t / P_t
out_t = P_t * S_t

whose state update is non-affine. The fold/fold_emit semantics is acc = a_t * acc + b_t with a_t, b_t fixed elementwise exprs; expressing the clamp needs a non-linear update function and a second coupled state — a semantics extension, not a vocabulary one. The atom must therefore stay ideal, and the corpus's clamped site is outside its reach.

Residue ​

  • Stabilized (clamped) scans — a non-affine, two-state recurrence; the affine fold grammar cannot close it (above).
  • log_softmax (a real invisible corpus op, 2 sites) — a normalization whose reduction result is wrapped in a unary (log . sum . exp) or a logsumexp reduction; the norm template covers a bare reduction operand only, and logsumexp has no torch binding.
  • Cost-neutral recognition: a certified law that fires without lowering extracted cost (the cumsum case) is an honest zero, not a bug — the atom's value would be its execution, which an ambient binding already provides.
