Retro — the $leaf: auto-compose seam + the batch_norm LHS law ​

Closes: the two named limits in project/adrs/0012-tensor-derives.md / GOALS.md §"Still open" — (a) $leaf: rules can't auto-compose in meta.synthesize_rules (the @i: placeholder machinery was attr-only), and (b) no law LHS named batch_norm (weightspace-fold.md gate 5 — content, not capability).

Item 1 — term-level placeholders ​

The ADR's resolver seam, landed as the twin of the attr machinery rather than a grammar widening. A $leaf:-produced RHS term metavar now travels through composition exactly the way a derived attr does:

  • _synthesizable admits a rule whose RHS term metavar is unbound when rule.derive can produce it (the attr clause's mirror; the earlier blanket return False was the measured refusal).
  • _fire_guarded keeps every derive output symbolic under the @i: namespace — "$attr:" keys become "@i:X" attr placeholders, bare ($leaf:) keys become "@i:X" leaf placeholders — and records the concrete value in conc either way.
  • _concretize_attrs resolves placeholder leaves (not just attr values) so a second parent's guards/derives see the minted Param/ Const, never the string.
  • _compose_guards namespaces term outputs identically: eff1["@1:X"] lets a pat2 entry reference the parent's derived leaf, and the composite derive emits out["@i:X"] — the instantiate-time resolution the ADR asked for, re-running the parent's spec through the pushed TensorAlgebra (source/ops/ mint) per firing. Nothing is baked in — the earlier silent bake of a concrete Param into a derived RHS (reachable only by cheating _synthesizable) is structurally impossible now.
  • _rhs_derive_placeholders scans leaf positions too, and _validate_candidate admits @i:-prefixed RHS term metavars when the candidate derives — unresolved placeholders veto the firing, which validation measures as refusal.

Nested composition namespaces correctly by construction ("@1:@2:X"), the same convention attr placeholders already use.

Measured. tests/test_batch_norm_expand.py: synthesize_rules([mark→marked(a,eps:$leaf:), marked(u,k)→sink(u,k)], [mark(v)]) emits a candidate whose RHS carries "@1:eps" and whose firing lands Const(2.0) — before the seam the parent was filtered by _synthesizable (refusal count 1 → 0). The twin seats are both covered ("@2:eps" when the $leaf: rule is r2), and batch_norm_expand itself — a real $leaf: rule — composes.

Item 2 — the batch_norm law family ​

laws/tensor.py gains BATCH_NORM_RULES — three arity variants of the eval-mode expansion batch_norm → γ·((x−μ)·rsqrt(σ²+ε)) + β (affine / weight-only / affine-free — the operand arities aten exports after dropping Nones). Everything is data:

  • cond: training=False (eval only — training computes batch stats, different math), concrete x/rm, channel-extent and per-channel shape agreement, 2 ≤ rank(x) ≤ 6.
  • dspec: CH = (1,) + shape(rm) + take(ones, rank(x)−2) — the (1, C, 1…) broadcast target derived per-rank, one law covering ranks 2–6 (take is Python-clamped; the ≤ 6 guard is what keeps a longer rank from minting a short target silently); $leaf:EPS mints Const(eps) through the scalar side of ADR 0012's channel — no tensor algebra needed.

Held out of DEFAULT as an opt-in list — the admission floor (the guarded sweep the promoted laws cleared) has not run; ALL_RULES is unchanged, extraction behavior for every existing caller unchanged. The fused member stays cheapest under extraction (priced 4 < the ~7-pass spelled subgraph), so the family is optionality, not a regression, wherever it is opted in.

Measured. fp64-exact vs F.batch_norm through the torch bridge (max|Δ| = 7.3e-13 on (2,6,4,4)); training=True, stats/x channel mismatch, rank-1 and rank-7 inputs, and unshaped x all decline — the strictness contract, never a fabricated member.

What stays open ​

  • The conv+BN weight fold itself: W' = W·γ·rsqrt(σ²+ε), b' = (b−μ)·scale + β — now reachable (stats are visible as terms, tval+mint can mint the folded Param), but the law is un-authored — it wants the tensor-valued $leaf: parents to auto-compose, which this seam enables; a follow-up measurement should try synthesize_rules/dspec_synth over a real conv→batch_norm seed rather than hand-writing it.
  • DEFAULT admission for BATCH_NORM_RULES — pending the guarded sweep (off this box's budget).
  • The mint-determinism contract remains documented-not-enforced (ADR 0012 (b)).
