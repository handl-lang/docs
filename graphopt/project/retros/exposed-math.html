Retro — the exposure screen: which models have e-graph-reachable math? ​

Date: 2026-10-15 Context: two measured lessons set this up. (1) project/retros/birefnet-compile-parity.md — BiRefNet delivered 0.80× vs compiled because its attention is a fused scaled_dot_product_attention call: a black box no law's LHS can see inside. (2) project/retros/retnet-external-baselines.md — RetNet delivered a real structural win because its retention was spelled elementwise. Discovery only pays where the model's math is visible to the IR, and nobody had screened the model zoo for that property. This retro builds the screen, ranks the corpus by it, and runs the discovery machinery on the most exposed models.

Headline. Exposure is a clean, bimodal property: of 321 exported workloads, 81 score exactly 0% FLOPs-weighted law coverage (the nn.* fused-op class — BiRefNet's class) while 140 score ≥95%. Exposure predicts reachability but not payoff: of the zoo's eight most exposed models, five fire shipped rules and four pay (ChunkedRetention −48%, LoRAAdapter −20%, GAU −9.5%, GCNLayer −7.7%); the other three score 100% and fire nothing. Running the full admission pipeline scoped to each model's own term found one genuinely unpublished machine-found rewrite that pays and verifies — census:mul_unsqueeze, minted by the census-naturality generator from DeltaRuleBlock's own mul(unsqueeze, unsqueeze) op-tuple: mul(unsq U d, unsq V d) = unsq(mul U V, d). It is relation=new, non-derivable, fires 4× (all typed), and drops the extracted cost −12.27% against the ALL_RULES baseline with a replaying certificate and a verified lowering. A strict-match sweep of the whole corpus shows the pattern exists only on DeltaRuleBlock — the new architecture generated a proposal no corpus term could.

The screen — lab/screen/exposure_screen.py ​

For each workload (23 zoo, ~250 intake candidates, 43 corpus models — 321 exported, 2 export-rejected), export to IR and measure:

  • lhs% — FLOPs-weighted share of op nodes whose op appears in at least one LHS pattern of the fireable surface (417 rules: ALL_RULES_WITH_LAYOUT + DEFAULT_RULES incl. carrier presets + ATTENTION + SCAN_LAWS/SCAN_DIAG_LAWS; 71 lhs-visible ops).
  • node% — the same, unweighted.
  • strict / rlx — subterms matching ≥1 LHS strictly / relaxed (repeated metavars as wildcards — "the shape is present, the equality precondition may not be").
  • invisible — the op census no LHS names (the black boxes), with FLOPs share. Unpriceable nodes (_INVALID_COST, e.g. the gather/scatter region of ExpertChoiceMoE) are excluded from the weights and counted in unp.
  • --fire N — a bounded saturation ground truth on the N most exposed terms (DEFAULT_RULES, 6 iters / 60k nodes).

The corpus picture (--set all) ​

The ranking is bimodal at the op level, not the architecture level. The 0%-FLOPs tail is exactly the fused-op class — every nn.* native that exports to a single opaque node (nn.LayerNorm, nn.Conv*, nn.*Pool*, nn.*Loss, nn.Embedding, nn.GRU*, nn.LSTM*, nn.Upsample*, nn.Bilinear, nn.CTCLoss-adjacent natives), plus hand workloads that are one fused call (ResNetBlock/TinyCNN/MBConvBlock/Bottleneck at 1–8%, DepthwiseConv* at 5.3%). These are the BiRefNet class: the e-graph literally cannot see the computation — there is nothing to rewrite. sdpa-spelling workloads (GQA-sdpa, SdpaBiasParam) are instructive edge cases: 100% coverage (the sdpa_fold family names sdpa in an LHS) but 0 strict matches — the op is law-visible, the fold's full shape is absent. Visible ≠ reachable.

Mid-range models are the interesting honest middle: LinearAttention (intake) at 51% — cumsum carries 44% of its FLOPs invisible; SelectiveScan/RunningNorm/CumsumScan/CumulativeGateRNN sit at 33–97% on cumsum/cumprod shares the laws can't touch; SinusoidalEncoding 69% (sin/cos invisible); SincKernel 79% (sinc/i0 invisible); DigammaLn/TrigHead/CoshAcosh ~33–60% on special-function tails.

Top of the whole corpus by exposure × size: TwoLayerHybrid (120 strict sites), SelectiveSSM (84), HybridBlock (68), DiagDenseSSM (60), TransformerBlock (52), DiagonalSSM (50) — all 100% — and DeltaRuleBlock (58 strict, 97.2%). The SSM/hybrid family is the most law-dense real architecture in the corpus, which matches the carriers' measured wins there.

Discovery on the exposed models — lab/screen/exposed_discovery.py ​

Three arms per model, all shipped machinery: (a) pipeline.propose scoped to the model's own op-tuple census → pipeline.measure → rank (the full admission gauntlet: oracle → typed gate → certificate → measured); (b) enumerate_candidates over the model's own op vocabulary, bounded oracle, impact._probe on every new+true candidate; (c) the 11 known unshipped machine laws probed. Ran on the top-3 by the screen (LoRAAdapter, GatedAttentionUnit, DeepEquilibrium) plus the four highest-site runners-up (AdaLNBlock, CosineAttention, CapsuleRouting, ChunkedRetention) and the new DeltaRuleBlock.

The find: census:mul_unsqueeze on DeltaRuleBlock ​

(mul (unsqueeze U, dim=D), (unsqueeze V, dim=D))
    = (unsqueeze (mul U, V), dim=D)

Minted by _census_naturality from DeltaRuleBlock's mul(unsqueeze, unsqueeze) op-tuple — the beta_t.unsqueeze(-1) * k_t.unsqueeze(-1) sites of the rank-1 write S += β·k ⊗ (v − kᵀS). Measured verdict: relation=new (no shipped law carries this two-sided naturality — mul_unsq_pad_{l,r} are strips, op(unsq u, v) → op(u, v)), not derivable from ALL_RULES, 4 strict matches / 4 typed fires / 0 ill-typed, paid=1, verify pass, certificate replays, closure 1.03×. Reach: extracted cost 992496 → 870723 = −12.27% over the ALL_RULES baseline (the full shipped DEFAULT_RULES alone gets only −3.5% on this term, via decay_state_lift_post/sub_to_add/matmul distribution). The only gate it fails is the view-oracle's guard isolation ("conditional truth: no single mechanical feature separates") — a candidate that is true on every real site it found but whose blanket form the guard machinery cannot certify. This is exactly the mul_unsq_pad admission story one family over: the law is real where the dims share, and needs a guard (or a same-rank operand precondition) the machinery cannot yet write.

A strict-match sweep of all 320 corpus terms: the pattern appears nowhere else — DeltaRuleBlock's 4 sites are the only mul(unsqueeze, unsqueeze) the corpus has ever seen. The new architecture contributed an op-tuple the proposal grammar could not have minted from the corpus.

Per-model, what fired / paid (pipeline arm) ​

  • DeltaRuleBlock (57 nodes, 97.2% exposed, 40 candidates): census:mul_unsqueeze above; mixed:mul_unsqueeze_r_w (16 fires, 9 typed — the β·outer site, conditional w:v_commutes_view); mixed:mul_unsqueeze_r_id (12 fires, conditional unsq:d_in_pad); mixed:sub_select_l_w — sub(select U,V) = select(sub U,V) — fires 4× but every fire mints an ill-typed member (select over broadcast shapes — a real guarded-law lead, correctly refused); mixed:sub_select_l_id fired 4× and the numeric oracle caught it false (the honest rejection working); sub_to_add_dup duplicate.
  • AdaLNBlock (41 candidates, 7 fire): grammar:mul_distribute is new, num_true, fires — and never lowers cost; six mixed:{mul,add}_chunk_* candidates fire on the triple-chunk conditioning site, all conditional (ck:single / w:v_commutes_view), three minting ill-typed members.
  • CosineAttention: reshape_transpose fires 3×, paid=1 — verify_fail=1: the known fake drop from model-zoo-yield.md, correctly refused.
  • CapsuleRouting: four mixed:mul_unsqueeze_* candidates fire (5 fires total), all conditional, 5 ill-typed mints.
  • ChunkedRetention: grammar:sub_to_add_dup fires once — library duplicate.
  • LoRAAdapter / GatedAttentionUnit / DeepEquilibrium: 35 candidates each, 0 fires — their shipped wins (assoc_linear, the om lifts) are not shapes the census/view/ grammar proposers mint; the proposal families can't see matmul associativity or carrier lifts.

Enumerate arm — an honest null, with a machinery finding ​

Across 8 models, the op-vocabulary enumerator emitted 74–400 candidates each (61 new + numerically-true on ChunkedRetention alone — contiguous/pull/hom forms over real ops like select, sigmoid, div). Zero fired on any model. The reason is structural and worth recording: the enumerator mints attr-free patterns (select X), but _term_match requires exact attr-key equality — select(x, dim=1, index=0) can never match a bare select pattern. The grammar's unary-op arm is blind to the attr-bearing ops real exports are made of. Candidates like select(add A B) = add(select A, select B) are the same naturality the mixed:*_select_*_w proposals found on DeltaRuleBlock — discovered independently, unable to fire.

Known-laws arm — also a null ​

The 11 unshipped machine laws (impact.new_laws() — mul-over-add factoring, neg distribution, the annihilators, x−x, x/x, …) fire on none of the eight models. They remain synthetic- witness laws: true, cheap, and absent from real spelled math.

The new architecture ​

zoo._DeltaRuleBlock (registered as DeltaRuleBlock): a DeltaNet- style unrolled delta-rule recurrence — S ← S + β·k ⊗ (v − kᵀS) — spelled elementwise over select/matmul/sub/add/mul/ unsqueeze/sigmoid. fp64, random-init, ingests clean through the real export boundary (57 nodes). Its efficient form — the chunked delta-rule scan — is not in the law library: the aff/affd scan monoids lift h′ = a·h + b, not a data-dependent (I − βkkᵀ) contraction on a matrix state (the shipped decay_state_lift_post does fire on it — a carrier law partially seeing the decayed structure — but only for −3.5%). At 97.2% FLOPs exposure and 58 strict LHS sites it is the most law-matchable term in the corpus per node, and it is the only model that minted a paying unpublished candidate this round.

Verdict ​

  • The exposure screen works as a filter: the 0%-tail (fused-op natives, conv stacks) is provably undiscoverable, the 100%-class is where discovery can see anything — and where payoff is still not guaranteed (DeepEquilibrium, AdditiveAttention, SoftSlotMoE score 100% and pay 0).
  • Unpublished machine-found rewrites this round: one that pays and verifies (census:mul_unsqueeze, −12.27%, DeltaRuleBlock, blocked at ship only by the guard-isolation gap), plus three conditional-truth leads (sub_select_l_w, mul_unsqueeze_r_w, mul_unsqueeze_r_id) in the same family, and grammar: mul_distribute (true, fires, pays nothing).
  • The honest nulls matter: the generic depth-2 enumerator is structurally unable to fire on real exported terms (attr-free patterns vs attr-bearing ops — fix = mint attr metavariables in the grammar's unary forms), and the 11 stored machine laws still fire nowhere real.
  • The discovery loop closed: a new spelled-out architecture → novel census op-tuple → machine-minted candidate → paid, verified, certified — and the admission gates correctly stopped it at "can't isolate the guard yet" rather than shipping a too-broad law.
