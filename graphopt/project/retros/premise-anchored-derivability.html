Retro — premise-anchored derivable: the composite's minting proof counts ​

Follows: binding-env-cap.md (its named policy question: "should corpus.base_rules carry a stored object's premise closure, so derivable reflects the premise-anchored proof?") and lemma-cert-admission.md. Commit: this commit's parent — evidence: premise-anchored derivability for stored composites.

The decision ​

Yes — with a site-verification guard. A stored composite's derivable leg now evaluates against corpus.base_rules plus its transitive premise closure: the record's guard.parents field resolved through the store member-wise — the same resolution admit_object performs for guard transport (ca42c16): each premise must be a row of the same store that rebuilds at full declared strength (missing_hooks minus transported empty), or — when it names a base rule — the axiom itself. A member that resolves short of that bar (no row, weaker-than-declared admit, cycle) empties the whole closure: a partial premise set is not the derivation the object was minted under.

The alternative — axioms-only derivable — is sound but blind: it under-reports a composite whose entire honest claim is "derivable under the premises I was minted from". The alternative the question warns against — unconditionally trusting the closure — is circular: verify_law's certificate proves each step is a valid application of a named rule, not that the named rule is true. A false stored rule in the closure would let a false composite fake derivability.

The anti-gaming bar ​

The anchored credit is granted only when the reported certificate is standalone-replayable and every step naming a non-axiom rule site-verifies: eval_instance(step.lhs, step.rhs) on the recorded concrete instance — the exact terms the premise rewrote between — under the same fp64 random-env oracle num_true uses. A premise true at the site the proof uses is sufficient (derivable is a per-instance verdict); a premise false at its applied site, an unverifiable step, or a non-replayable witness all revoke the credit — measure tries base_rules first, so the revoked verdict falls back to the honest axiom-only answer. The flag surfaces as Evidence.premise_anchored and derivable=True(premise-anchored) in the truth-gate detail.

What the tests pin ​

tests/test_discovery_gauntlet.py (+6): the flip on a minted square→pow composite under an axiom-thin corpus; transitivity through a stored syn_* intermediate; refusal when a premise row is absent or admits weaker than declared; axiom-parents resolving from base_rules without a store row; a false-premise pair that merges the e-graph yet fails site verification; and the audit's unit edges (no cert, non-replayable, axioms-only).

What stays open ​

  • The conv+BN composite's closure refusal (6.00× > 2.0 cap) — expansion-composite policy, not derivability.
  • premise_anchored does not reach the verdicts table: the gauntlet does not record verdict rows, so nothing persists a stale-anchored derivable; a future cached-verdict path for stored objects must carry the anchor scope in its key.
