Retro — _MAX_BINDING_ENVS: the composite gauntlet terminates, honestly ​

Date: 2026-10-19. Follows: enablement-floor.md (its named scaling gap: "run_gauntlet/pl.measure on guarded minted composites does not terminate within the box") and fold-cost-single-source.md. Commit: this commit (oracle._MAX_BINDING_ENVS, the probe's gauntlet leg, two pins in test_discovery_oracle.py).

The hotspot, corrected ​

The retro named the 83-rule _reach/derivability saturation. Measured (faulthandler stacks + per-stage timers on the stored conv+BN composite, one-case corpus):

stagemeasured
verify_law (derivability, 83 rules)0.0 s — 54 enodes, not derivable
_probe / _typed_probe (lone rule)0.0 s each — 29 enodes
_reach (_saturate ×2, 83/84 rules, budgeted)0.0 s — 9→54 enodes, fixed_point
sweep_real0.0 s — one real match
oracle.synthesize (verify_view_candidate)never returned — the whole hang

The explosion is not the e-graph; it is the binding-env enumeration. synthesize, evidence._synth_sites and object_synthesis._oracle_sites all cap the sites they yield — but a derive veto or an uninstantiable pair consumes an env without producing a site. The composite is a 4-deep syn_* composition whose composed derive declined 2000/2000 enumerated envs at ~8k envs/s on a domain orders past 10⁹ envs — every cap the consumers held was on the output side of a faucet that never dripped. This is the same failure class _MAX_VIEWED_BINDINGS closed one level down; the missing bound was on the env stream itself.

The fix ​

oracle._binding_envs now stops after _MAX_BINDING_ENVS = 250_000 yielded envs — a property of the enumeration machinery, so every consumer (synthesize, _synth_sites, _oracle_sites) inherits the bound unchanged. Sizing is measured, not guessed: the largest legitimate sweep observed is sdpa_fold_nomask's at ~180k envs to natural exhaustion under synthesize (its derive vetoes ~2 200:1 — the shipped worst case), so 250k preserves every shipped sweep with headroom while bounding a veto-everything domain to a fixed corner of the Cantor order — a partial sweep, the same honesty the instance and viewed-binding caps already document. (rms_norm_fold's leaf-only sweep measures 13.9M envs/134 s to exhaustion — it has no viewish op, so synthesize is unreachable for it through measure; the cap now bounds it too if it is ever swept directly.)

The measured verdict ​

conv_fold_premise_pay_probe.py now runs run_gauntlet end to end on the one-case corpus: 4.9 s (was: >9 min, unbounded). Stages: reconstruct ✓, full-data ✓ (guard transport), measure ✓, truth FAIL — numeric=True, view=true (the one evaluable instance agrees), but the guarded synth region is starved (0 accepted / 2000 declined / 2360 envs, escalation included) and derivable=False under corpus.base_rules (ALL_RULES — the premise closure is not in the search set). The refusal is the honest starvation verdict the gate documents: the enumeration bank cannot mint the conv-channel corner — the same "corner-reaching truth route" already named open — not a wrong answer and not a hang.

Pay leg (unchanged, now confirmed under the gauntlet's own measure): fires=1 changed=True verified=pass, dag_cost fused 17 438.7 vs spelled 34 874.3 — the FOLD_OPS single-sourcing made the composite a payer.

What stays open ​

  • The truth gate's synth-side starvation for stored composites: the binding banks have no conv-weight-shaped inhabitants. The named fix remains a corner-reaching enumeration vocabulary (or witness-driven sites) — the cap bounds the work, it does not move the corner.
  • A stored composite's premises are outside corpus.base_rules, so derivable reads False where the premise-anchored replay (verify_law under ALL_RULES + closure) proves it. Whether the gauntlet corpus should carry the record's premise closure is a policy question, not a bound.

Gate account: tests/test_discovery_oracle.py (+2 env-cap pins), test_law_view_oracle.py, test_discovery_{gauntlet,evidence,pipeline, autocond,dspec_synth,synthesis,arena,meta_arena,enumerate,climb_b}, test_machine_pack.py, test_construction_gaps.py, test_conv_fold_rules.py, test_batch_norm_expand.py, test_guard_sweep.py — all green (the one failure, test_every_conditional_rule_is_swept 125≠124, is pre-existing at bb6a2c4 — verified with the fix stashed). ruff/format/ty clean on touched files.
