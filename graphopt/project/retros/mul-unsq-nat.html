Retro: admitting census:mul_unsqueeze — the guard question, resolved ​

Context: exposed-math-discovery.json recorded the exposure agent's best find — mul(unsqueeze U d, unsqueeze V d) = unsqueeze(mul U V, d) on DeltaRuleBlock: new, non-derivable, 4 typed fires, −12.27% (992496→870723), verify pass, certificate replays — blocked only by "conditional truth (view-oracle): no single mechanical feature separates". This retro records the admission decision: what the guard is, why the machine-minted clause was not it, and what shipped.

The truth region ​

unsq(x, d) inserts a size-1 axis at d mod (rank+1); broadcasting right-aligns. Pushing mul through a shared insertion is the naturality mul(unsq u d, unsq v d) = unsq(mul u v, d). The semantic boundary is exact and mechanical:

  • equal ranks — always true (both operands insert at the same normalized position; well-typedness of either side is the same condition: bcast(u,v) must resolve);
  • unequal ranks — true iff each operand's flat-read coefficient map agrees across the two orders. The shorter operand's inserted axis lands at d + (R − r) in the result grid vs the RHS's d mod (R+1); inserts inside the broadcast pad (d ≤ 0 region) commute, mid-rank inserts do not — e.g. u=(2,), v=(1,2,2), d=1 equalizes the grids (1,1,2,2) both ways but reads u[i] vs u[j].

Adversarial sweep (pure IR semantics — mod-normalized dims, numpy broadcast; ranks ≤3, extents {1,2,3}, d ∈ −8..8): 12956 evaluable equal sites, 2464 unequal, 1388 rhs-err. Verdicts:

guardbad-acceptsequal-missed
rank-eq(u,v) ∧ shaped(bcast u v)06156 (~48%)
grid-eq only — bcast-eq(unsq-out u d, unsq-out v d, unsq-out(bcast u v) d, ·)2120
flat-map-unsq(u, UD, v, unsq-out(v, UD))00

flat-map-unsq — the wl-naturality predicate minted for mul(unsq u K, reshape v S) = unsq(mul u v, K) — is the exact region when its partner-shape argument S is instantiated with the partner's own unsq-out spec: it checks grid coincidence AND both operands' flat-read maps. That is the shipped guard — one clause, pure DSL data, serializable.

What auto_cond minted — and why it is not the guard ​

auto_cond_object on the bare pattern (synth_limit 360, 174 measured envs: 69 equal / 16 bad) minted a single clause — the grid-eq bcast-eq above — separating its measured domain perfectly and unsound past it: the synthesized env bank never produced the unequal-rank mid-insert corner (the sweep shows 212 bad-accepts). The stock _pred_bank emits flat-map-unsq only with reshape-out partner specs — the unsq-out instantiation the two-sided law needs is outside the minted vocabulary, so the search picked the best available clause and the corner is unmeasured, not unguardable. Honest lesson for the bank: flat-map-unsq's S position should also range over unsq-out specs when the partner view is unsqueeze.

The sibling: mixed:mul_unsqueeze_r_id — already admitted ​

mul(V, unsqueeze(U,d)) → mul(U,V) is α-identical to the shipped mul_unsq_pad_r (promoted-laws.md row 6) — the same unsq:d_in_pad family. Corpus-wide tally of its 22 strict-match sites / 12 models:

  • 2 sites accepted by the shipped guard (intake:BroadcastPadRight, zoo:TimeCondConv) — true pad strips;
  • 10 rejected sites are numerically unequal — including all 4 DiagDenseSSM sites, where V binds b_eye: I·unsq(U,−1) and U·I coincide only because the identity masks off-diagonals (diag(U) both ways) — a contextual equality, not a law. The −14.3% "pay" measured there was the false direction made invisible by eye-masking; unsq:d_in_pad rejecting d=−1 (trailing insert, not a pad) was the correct refusal;
  • 10 rejected sites unevaluable (err);
  • 0 rejected-but-equal — the shipped guard loses nothing.

So the same guard resolution already unlocks it: the store object stays unshipped as a store object because the law it names is the law that shipped.

What shipped ​

mul_unsq_nat → SIMPLIFICATION_RULES (default-on; the pad-strip family's home):

mul(unsq u UD, unsq v UD) -> unsq(mul u v, UD)
cond = ("flat-map-unsq", "u", "UD", "v", ("unsq-out", "v", "UD"))

Closure measurement (7-model slice, lone-rule reach): zero enode growth off-site; DeltaRuleBlock +1.3% enodes (558→565), −12.3% cost, add_cert=pass, fixed-point. The default set is justified — no speculative e-node bloat, real pay on-site.
