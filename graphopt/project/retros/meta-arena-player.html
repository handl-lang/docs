Retro — the meta-arena player (Q3, first answer) ​

Plan 0021's Q3: is the mixed board deep enough that a learned player beats the scripted saturate → extract playbook? The machinery is landed — catopt_discovery.meta_player is a linear softmax policy over meta_arena.legal_actions with an exact (uncapped) softmax, played-move masking, ε-exploration, and reward-to-go REINFORCE; the schema is data (lawdata.META_ARENA_FEATURES). Reproduce:

.venv/bin/python -m catopt_discovery.meta_player \
    --train-cases 1000 --eval-cases 20 --budget 24 \
    --temperature 1.2 --epsilon 0.25 --json run.json

The paying line exists — verified by hand ​

The Q2 probe's baseline table already showed the headroom: scripted/greedy/random all tie at baseline on every demo case. What they miss is a four-move composition: declare a fresh fold of a mined spec → saturate so the minted rule fires → handle to bind a supported kernel → extract to collect. Hand-set weights prove the schema expresses it: silu sites go 2→1, swiglu 3→1, nested 4→3, controls and infeasible-kernel boards correctly decline. test_winning_line_reachable pins it.

The measurement — cold RL does not find it (at 1000 episodes) ​

gen_cases mixes paying boards (silu/swiglu sites under sans = DEFAULT - {silu_fold, softsign_fold}, kernel still in the ambient vocabulary) with non-paying ones (softsign under sans — kernel out of vocab; controls under DEFAULT).

Train decade means climb steadily (−0.94 → −0.41, plateau ~−0.44) — the learner is doing real work: op:extract +3.47, op:fire −2.54. What it converged to is the stoppable-time policy: extract immediately, skip the wasteful saturate. That honestly beats scripted on mean reward — but on declared-use columns it wins nothing.

Why the line is hard — and what this measures ​

The net payout for the composed line is ~+0.25 over immediate-extract; per-step cost −0.05 makes the margin thin, and on 3/5 board kinds the line does not exist at all — a policy that learns "declare usually doesn't pay" is locally correct. This is the sparse-reward credit-assignment wall: the memoryless linear policy cannot plan a composition. Two features were added on the way and confirmed load-bearing-weak: st:unhandled/x:unhandled:handle (declared-but-unhandled is exactly where handle pays) and st:handleable/x:handleable: declare (a mined spec whose canon form a handler covers — the precondition, computable from MetaState data alone).

Verdict and next levers ​

Q3 is answered in the negative at this budget: a cold-start linear REINFORCE policy beats the scripted baseline on reward but only via the degenerate line — the composed strategy is in the policy class (hand weights reach it) yet unreachable by sampling at ~10³ episodes. The construction-arena result transfers: learning beat baselines where paying moves were dense; here they are composed, and composition is what the policy lacks.

Levers, in order of how well they match the tower's own thesis:

  1. Options/macros — landed. claim(tag) is the composed line as one refereed move (declare a covered spec → fire its rule → bind the interpretation; Sanada's handle (declare spec) with H fused). With it in the legal set the line compresses to claim → extract: sampling reachability is fixed — the 300-episode run found 4 structural wins (silu 2→1) where zero existed before. Final reading — 800-episode run over occurrence-gated claims: learned −0.03 mean reward, 3 certified structural wins on 40 held-out cases (swiglu 3→1, silu 2→1, swiglu 3→2) — beats scripted (−0.10, zero structural wins) on both axes. Caveat: the wins ride sampling variance, not learned preference — op:claim's weight sits ≈+0.04, learned-greedy never claims. The board question is answered: a learned player beats scripted on the mixed board, because the option move compressed the composition — action-space structure is what the board was missing; consolidation by a memoryless linear policy remains the open gap for a stronger policy class.
  2. Curriculum — partially landed. Training on the paying-board subset (claimable > 0; 250 of 400 generated) shifts the policy measurably: the stochastic arm's eval declared-wins tripled (9 of 40 incl. swiglu/silu sites and a control board with a paying subterm), mean cost 2.73 < scripted's 2.92 — but mean reward degrades (−0.35) because the policy over-claims where the extraction then can't improve. Curriculum concentrates the gradient on the interesting boards; it does not teach when to stop.
  3. Imitation warm-start — behavior-clone the known line, then RL. Honest about which part is discovery vs consolidation.
  4. More training — 10⁴+ episodes; the decade trend was still rising at plateau's edge.

Housekeeping ​

  • lawdata.META_ARENA_PLAYER_WEIGHTS stays empty — no trained snapshot ships until one earns it (the eval arms re-train in main; nothing memorized).
  • The ε knob and reward-to-go are learner machinery, not board changes — the reward table is untouched.
