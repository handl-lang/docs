Retro: the vocabulary week — originality found, delivery dropped ​

Window: 2026-10-19 18:12 (pitch tag v0.1.0) → 2026-10-25 Context: six days after the audited positioning deck, the stack moved further than the deck can express. This retro is the delta in one place: what grew, what was learned, what measurably broke, and what that forces next. Every claim names its artifact; anything not named here is not done.

2. The finding — originality is a function of the vocabulary ​

docs/reports/primitives_finds.md (2026-10-23, zoo-prim, 68 modules):

  • 21/21 admitted prim folds classify machine-original, because the op is new — "novel by construction, since no catalog contains laws about an op that did not exist".
  • --prim-deep: 48/48 admitted, all machine-original, disjoint names. --prim-bootstrap seats the shipped folds in the corpus e-graph as vocabulary (23 fired, +475 enodes) and 2 depth-2 folds become reachable; the depth-3 tier then lands 34 more.
  • Content: DeltaNet, Gated Linear Attention, Mamba selective scan and Griffin's RG-LRU share one "sum of gated products" core; one primitive (bb8c54c201) binds 14 modules. Found by corpus antiunification, not by being told.

The control, measured: the codegen domain's 44-case deep run mints 117, admits 97, and classifies 0 machine-original — composed rules whose parents are both in the bank are theorems of the bank. So:

Originality appears exactly where the vocabulary may grow, and vanishes where composition inside a fixed bank is all the mint can express.

That sentence is now the thesis, and it is two-sided. It also re-opens and re-answers Q1: the honest zero on zoo-real (2026-10-21, 53 modules, 87 mints, 0 originals) was not search starvation — it was a vocabulary wall. The lever was never "guarded composite parents".

3. The catch — the pay leg was priced, never measured ​

docs/reports/prim_delivery.md (2026-10-24). The synop* family ships in DEFAULT; shipped_primitives.register() seats a Python closure that walks the definitional premise per node.

  • measured wall clock, fp64 eager, small and large: 13 of 14 cells lose or tie (marginal 1.04–1.95×, e2e 1.07–2.20×); the compiled twins agree. The single "delivers" cell routes to a different executor — a routing artifact, not a dispatch win.
  • mechanism: the spec-walk costs more than the N−1 dispatches it removes, at every measured size. There is no fused kernel.
  • consequence for the ledger: the pay leg for this family was cost-model only. The referee priced the move and the price was wrong about the executor.

This is now tracked as tech debt (TODO → "synop* real delivery") and it is the sharpest open problem in the domain: a referee that prices can be gamed by its own cost model.

Update, same night — the diagnosis was right. prim_kernel_probe gave six of the pointwise prims a generated triton.jit body and timed it against the shipped binding on CUDA fp32 (1 M elements): 0.371–0.392× the binding — 2.55–2.70× faster, every op verified (graphopt/docs/reports/prim_kernel.md). So the fold never was slow; the interpreter was. The shipped path is fp64 on CPU, where Triton cannot run, which is why the module-level delivery probe sees a loss.

4. A second domain, and the referee that measures ​

kernelopt/ (private; numbers do not leave it) — paper/paper.qmd, docs/results_kernel_check.md, docs/results_overnight_kernel_v2.md.

  • 26 codegen laws; three-legged referee: certificate, verify-first numeric equality in the sink, and measured wall time against a hand-Triton field baseline.
  • 11-case audit, CUDA fp32: verified-equal fused composites 1.1–5.0× over spelled, at-or-near the Triton rows; kernel_flat control 1.00×; two mm-epilogue rows drifted, ordering holds.
  • learned linear player beats the scripted playbook, held-out: 0.560 vs 0.200 reward/episode.
  • headline: a 145-node carrier the sink could not lower at all → a learned schedule fold emits a fused chunked-scan plan at 0.271 ms vs 0.454 ms incumbent (~0.28 ms FLA, carried retro).
  • caveats carried in the paper: realised under CUDA-graph replay (eager is Python-bound); parity on a launch-bound term.

Reading: the domain whose referee times produces delivery wins; the domain whose referee prices produces them on paper only. Same game layer, same seat, different score.

5. The boundary — priors do not move worlds ​

kernelopt/docs/results_transfer.md (Q5 remainder):

  • 10 of 12 foreign policy/domain pairs cannot enumerate a single legal move; the enumerators are structurally typed to their own state.
  • the open vocabulary shares zero feature names with any other domain.
  • the one live channel is a:r:*, the rule-name hash bucket — same hash, different vocabularies; search → kernel reaches 96.8 % live mass and is semantically scrambled by construction.
  • every transplant ≤ 0.39× of the domain's own prior; native kernel greedy +0.653 vs cold-greedy −0.129.

So Q5 is NULL, sharpened: what transfers is the game (world, referee, score, seat); what does not is the vocabulary and featurization. A prior is a function of a featurization.

7. Architecture — the two decisions the data forced ​

  • ADR-0157 / plan 0033 — the fragment tower. Vocabularies become Language fragments; minting is fragment emission; growth is a fragment merge. Phases 0–5 in project/plans/0033-fragment-tower.md, each shipping standalone value. Phase 0 (canonical fragment dir + emit API) has no external dependency and is the generator fix. Stated falsifier: if the flagship stack cannot live on the fragment tower, the language-as-data claim is unproven on its hardest corpus.
  • ADR-0158 — effects resume in flight. perform dispatches to the innermost covering handler and its return value is the result; the old trace semantics become the unhandled case; stateful handlers ride the calculus' own loop/trace; multi-shot resume deferred.
  • Supporting: ADR-0154 (typecheck semantics on an NBE engine), ADR-0152 (nominal inductive families), ADR-0153 (grades as arrow indices), reify_ast (Val→Ast quote half), CI off Dagger onto the bupha runner with smoke/verify layers.
  • Artifacts: platform paper (eqsat/paper, 2026-10-21), tensor-domain paper (paper/), private codegen-domain paper.

9. What we are not claiming ​

  • not "127 laws": 127 ledger names = 13 promoted + 57 folds + 57 premises.
  • not a speed win for the minted family — the opposite, measured.
  • not a reusable player — the transfer test says no.
  • not that the self-modifying game has been shown to improve play. What is shown: the bank grows under play, and a player ranks candidates 4.7× better than the heuristic. The loop between them — play grows the bank, the grown bank makes the same player better without retraining — is still unmeasured. That is the G* claim, and it remains open.

10. What next — ranked by risk removed per week ​

  1. Plan 0033 Phase 0 — canonical fragment vocabulary (no external dependency). Emit ops/laws/handlers/schemas/prims/families from their five current owners behind one schema pin; registries become projections. Pass: an added fragment (human or machine) is visible to pipeline.propose and mlopt-handl-peer.transform_ids identically.
  2. Make pay measured, then re-admit. Either build the fused-body codegen path for minted ops (genkernel already lowers claimed handlers) or gate MACHINE_SHIPPED on measured pay. Landed 2026-10-25: bench.referees.pay_wall (graphopt 523ab1d) times the delivered module, with a same-executor rule. Verdict: 7 lose · 1 tie · 6 confounded · 0 honest delivers, and every fold that fires (9 of 57) loses, worst 2.66× (docs/reports/pay_measured.md). So the pass criterion is met the honest way — the family is demoted as a pay claim, kept as provenance. Still open: the 48 unmeasured folds, and whether a real fused body can beat Inductor at these sizes (the compiled twins say no).
  3. The decisive experiment: does a grown bank improve the same player? Freeze a player, grow the bank, replay the frozen player on the same held-out boards. Pass: a delta with a paired control. Measured 2026-10-25 (bench/drivers/policy/bank_growth.py, docs/results_bank_growth.md, 5 seeds, frozen linear player):
    • minted prim growth 74 → 131: −0.023 reward/episode (all seeds ≤ +0.008) — unpaid moves dilute a fixed policy;
    • control, paying growth 127 → 131 (id_add/id_mul/double_neg/ silu_fold): +0.066 (all seeds ≥ +0.036), ~9× the reward. So growth helps iff it pays — the same condition the measured pay gate enforces. G* is not "the bank grows"; it is "the bank grows with moves that pay". Still to run: mint during play, then measure.
  4. Held-out replication of 21/21. Mint on zoo-prim, admit on a model family never in the mint corpus. Pass: originals survive at a stated rate, or the corpus dependence is named.
  5. Close the tech debt that bank growth will break: import-time domain registration, pinned rule-count tests, kernel-prim shape gate. Cheap now, expensive at 200 rules.
  6. Un-pin the delivery path from CPU. torch_bridge.Plan.run_xs allocates intermediates from a CPU like tensor, so a generated Triton body cannot be measured end to end — the op-level 2.6× stands, the module-level number does not exist yet. measure_module now takes device/dtype; the harness (prim_kernel_module) is written and blocked on that one change. This is the highest-value refactor left: it turns "the folds pay" from an op-level result into a delivered one.

Decision on the 48 unmeasured folds (2026-10-25): accept them as vocabulary, not as pay claims. They are labeled unmeasured in provenance.pay_status() and in docs/reports/pay_coverage.md; measuring each on its own bound site is a follow-up, not a blocker, because a fold that never fires cannot pay on the corpus that matters.

Deferred on purpose: scale (Q4 already names the three walls — matcher ~11k nodes, verify-forward RAM, fp64 overflow), and any new domain until the pay leg is honest.

11. One-line summary ​

The machine can now name new things and prove them — 127 names, 21 of 21 original — and the only two things still missing are a vocabulary that lives as data and a referee that measures instead of prices.
