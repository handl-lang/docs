Retro — conv_channel_scale anchor landed; the fold mints by ITERATED composition ​

Follows: conv-bn-fold-synth.md (named the missing conv2d-headed anchor, gate A). Commit: 945733f (CONV_FOLD_RULES in mlopt_core.laws.tensor, opt-in with BATCH_NORM_RULES). Probe: lab/probes/conv_bn_fold_synth_probe.py — re-run, gates A/B/B3/C/D.

The anchor — CONV_FOLD_RULES (60 rules, all data) ​

The conv twin of linear_channel_scale, plus its shift sibling — the absorb half of the conv+BN weight-space pair:

conv2d(x, W, b) · ŝ  =  conv2d(x, W∘ŝ′, b∘s)      ŝ ~ (1, C, 1, 1)
conv2d(x, W, b) ± ĉ  =  conv2d(x, W, b ± c)       ĉ ~ (1, C, 1, 1)
  • Per-output-channel scale folds through W's axis 0 AND the bias; the per-channel shift folds into the bias outright (conv is linear in its filter). linear's channel scale folds the weight's INPUT axis — conv's folds the OUTPUT axis.
  • The sv/cv metavar binds any term whose shape is the channel view — the fold site carries compounds (γ̂·r̂, ŝ·(−μ̂)+β̂), never a lone reshape. The (C,1,1,1) weight view WV and (C,) bias vector SV are dspec-derived reshapes.
  • Guards (data): batched rank-4 conv2d only; x/W/sv concrete; sv == (1, W[0], 1, 1); F.conv2d well-typedness x[1] == W[1]·G (dim-eq when the export elides groups, attr-cmp×mul when spelled).
  • Generated per (attr-prefix × bias-arity × operand-order × direction): conv2d's positional tail is a prefix of (stride, padding, dilation, groups) and match_pattern requires an exact attr-key set — 6 shapes × 5 prefixes × 2 arities, the _make_sdpa_fold_rules/SPLIT_SCALAR_RULES precedent.
  • Verified fp64 against F.conv2d incl. groups=2 (tests/test_conv_fold_rules.py, 10 tests). Opt-in CONV_FOLD_RULES — held out of ALL_RULES/DEFAULT with the BN family pending the admission floor.

Probe re-run — the measured account ​

Gate A — corpus gap closed. conv2d: 0 → 60 lhs + 60 rhs entries. Gate B — pairwise synthesize_rules still refuses the bare fold: rhs_heads_conv2d is False in every bank (DEFAULT+BN, ALL+BN, ALL+BN+CONV, ALL+BN+CONV+PW), fold_composites empty. But the anchor participates immediately: syn_batch_norm_expand__ conv_channel_shift_sub_sp_6 mints batch_norm(conv2d(…)) → add(mul(γ̂, mul(conv2d(x,W,b−μ̂), r̂)), β̂) — the running mean absorbed into the conv bias in ONE pairwise composite.

Gate B3 — iterated composition mints it. synthesize_rules composes pairs over a FIXED usable list (derivation depth exactly 2; emitted composites never re-enter the pool). Feeding each round's emitted set into the next round's bank — usable = base + emitted — the fold mints in 4 rounds (emitted 26 → 61 → 242 → 944 on fuel=30000):

syn_…_batch_norm_expand__conv_channel_shift_sub_sp_6
    __conv_channel_scale_sp_48__conv_channel_scale_l_sp_209
    __conv_channel_shift_sp_896

i.e. batch_norm(conv2d(x,W,b),γ,β,μ,σ²) → conv2d(x, W′, b′) — the chain expand → shift_sub → scale → scale_l → shift. Verified fp64-exact on the real seed: max diff 6.8e-13 vs the fused kernel (env bound from source_tensors — the randn env NaNs on rv<0, gate B2). W′ spells W·rsqrt(σ²+ε)·γ and b′ (b−μ̂)·ŝ·γ̂ + β̂ through the derived CH/WV/SV views.

Gates C/D unchanged — dspec_synth mints the derives staged (scale/b′ depth 3, W′ depth 2 given scale); the one-shot tensor-tier W′ still refuses on depth ≥4 + (C,)×(C,i,k,k) broadcast.

What stays open (named, ordered) ​

  1. Composition depth is the refusal, not material. A synthesize_rules fixpoint knob — feed emitted composites back into usable until convergence or a round cap (the probe's gate-B3 loop is the reference; a rounds= parameter or a driver in mlopt_discovery) — or the ADR-0008 anchored mint (antiunify over a measured seed↔folded pair) which jumps the depth directly.
  2. The emitted fold's guard content. The minted composite carries the transported conjunction of all five parents' conds; whether it serializes clean through law_to_data/rulecache and what its derived-attr placeholder chain looks like at admission time is unmeasured.
  3. CONV_FOLD_RULES DEFAULT/ALL_RULES admission — the guarded sweep (bn-admission-sweep.md posture), now that the fold actually composes on a real seed.
  4. Staged dspec_synth + a channel-broadcast mul/tapp in the enumeration bank — the gate-D sub-gaps, unchanged.

Caveats measured ​

  • numeric_check=True still drops the BN composites — the _tensor_env rv<0 → rsqrt NaN artifact (gate B2); the iterated gate runs numeric_check=False and verifies the fold separately on the real tensors.
  • The _spdg groups attr-cmp reads x[1] == W[1]·GR — the only clause in the family that multiplies a bound attr; a non-int GR declines (tensor-tier _tcall wraps the error).
