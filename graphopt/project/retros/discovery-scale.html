Retro — discovery at corpus scale: sweeping the exposed class ​

Date: 2026-10-14 Context: project/retros/exposed-math.md screened 321 workloads for e-graph reachability — 140 score ≥95% FLOPs-weighted law coverage — and ran the three-arm discovery machinery on eight of them, minting one paying unpublished law (census:mul_unsqueeze, −12.27% on DeltaRuleBlock). Nobody had run discovery across the rest of the exposed class. This retro runs lab/screen/discovery_scale.py — a budgeted sweep driver over exposed_discovery.py's arms — on the top 60 eligible models and asks the corpus-level question the project thesis lives on: how much unpublished paying structure actually exists in the exposed class?

Headline. Across 60 newly swept high-exposure models (58 full runs + 2 truncated at the 240 s per-model budget, rerun with the enumerate oracle skipped): zero genuinely-new paying unpublished laws. The machinery minted 2,101 pipeline proposals, measured 527 new-and-true candidates, fired on 44 models — and the only paying hits are (a) exact re-derivations of two already-banked unshipped machine laws (cand_mul_factor, cand_mul_factor_right), and (b) one rule derivable from ALL_RULES. All four paying rows land on purpose-built intake probes — micro-models constructed to contain the structure. On organic architectures: nothing new pays. mul_unsqueeze remains the only unpublished machine-minted law that pays — 1 find in 68 swept exposed models (~1.5%).

The sweep — lab/screen/discovery_scale.py ​

Selection: screen rows with lhs_cover ≥ 0.95, minus the eight already-run models (DONE — LoRAAdapter, GatedAttentionUnit, DeepEquilibrium, CosineAttention, AdaLNBlock, CapsuleRouting, ChunkedRetention, DeltaRuleBlock), ranked by lhs_cover × strict_sites. 133 eligible; top 60 taken — covering the entire strict-sites ≥ 2 head plus the named-architecture tail.

Budgets: --per-model 240 (a real SIGALRM wall-clock per model — overrun records truncated and the sweep moves on), a 3000 s total sweep budget, a 1.5 GB MemAvailable abort floor, and the enumerator's --max-candidates 400 / --evaluate 200 prefix caps unchanged. Results stream to project/retros/discovery-scale.json after every model. Name collisions across registries (SwiGLU/LinearAttention/ConvNeXtBlock exist in two sources) are handled by source:name qualified selection, added to exposed_discovery._cases_by_name; evidence rows now carry term_to_data serializations (lhs_data/rhs_data) so the parent sweep can rebuild candidate terms for the corpus-novelty pass.

The known-laws arm independently confirms the overlap: cand_mul_factor fires + pays (52,213 → 34,809, −33.3%) on QuadraticFeature and SharedFactorMixture; cand_mul_factor_right pays the same on SharedFactorMixtureRight. Both arms converged on identical terms — cross-validated by bidirectional _term_match in discovery_scale._known_law_of.

The caveat that matters: all four paying rows are PURPOSE_BUILT intake workloads — probes literally constructed to contain add(mul·, mul·) sharing a factor or a noop transpose. The corpus strict-match novelty sweep confirms it: factor_left's LHS exists on exactly those 2 models, factor_right's on 1, add_transpose_l_id's on 2 (NoopTransposeAdd + ViTPatchBlock). These are the synthetic- witness patterns of round 1 — except now measured paying on the probes that were built for them. That is validation of the bank, not discovery of new structure.

The conditional-truth lead worth recording ​

mixed:mul_unsqueeze_r_id — mul(V, unsqueeze(U, dim)) = mul(U, V) — fires 4× (all typed, 0 ill-typed) on model:DiagDenseSSM and pays −14.3%, blocked (correctly) at the same gate as mul_unsqueeze: num_true=None, guard unsq:d_in_pad — the view machinery cannot isolate when the unsqueeze dim is a pad/broadcast. It is the same two-sided unsqueeze-naturality family as the DeltaRuleBlock find — and unlike mul_unsqueeze (LHS on exactly 1 model), its LHS mul(V, unsq U) strict-matches 12 corpus models / 22 sites (DeltaRuleBlock 8, DiagDenseSSM 4, plus GCNLayer, CapsuleRouting, AdditiveAttention, ExpertChoiceMoE, FNOBlock, TimeCondConv, and 4 intake terms): a general guarded-law lead, not a one-architecture idiom. mixed:mul_transpose_l_id pays −2% under conditional truth on the transformer-block family (ConformerBlock, Pre/PostNormBlock, nn.MultiheadAttention). None are shipped; all are recorded with evidence.

The honest nulls, reproduced at scale ​

  • Enumerate arm: 1,263 new+true candidates, 0 fires across all 60 models. The structural blindness round 1 identified is not a small-sample artifact: attr-free patterns (select X) can never match attr-bearing real ops (select(x, dim=1, index=0)). The enumerator keeps minting true laws that cannot fire on exported IR. The fix is still: mint attr metavariables in the grammar's unary forms.
  • False-candidate rejection works at scale. The numeric oracle killed grammar:FALSE_mul_factor (a·b + a·c = a·(a+c) — a subtle-wrong factor twin that would have shown a −33.3% "drop"), exp(add) = add(exp, exp), square(add) = add(square, square), and every *_id transpose/select overreach — 14 verify-fails and 72 ill-typed mints refused, zero false positives in the paying set.
  • Truncation cause identified: the two truncated models (LinearRecurrence, KernelizedAttention) burn the budget inside the enumerate arm's bounded oracle — grammar._evaluate runs a small e-graph saturation (proposal._sat_cost/_cost_delta) per candidate, ~0.35 s × 200 candidates, plus pathological cases past that. With --evaluate 0 both complete in <5 s (both null: LinearRecurrence mints one numeric-false + one conditional select-naturality, KernelizedAttention fires nothing). Pipeline + known-arms coverage is complete on all 60.

The corpus-level answer ​

  • Swept: 68 exposed models total (8 round 1 + 60 here) — the strict-match head of the ≥95% class.
  • Genuinely unpublished paying laws: 1 — mul_unsqueeze (−12.27%, DeltaRuleBlock). Rate ≈ 1/68 ≈ 1.5% of the exposed class at these budgets.
  • Unpublished-and-paying counting bank re-derivations: 3 distinct laws on 5 models (~7%) — but every bank hit is on a purpose-built probe. On organic architectures the count stays 1.
  • Typical or lucky? The sweep says mul_unsqueeze was neither a fluke nor typical — it is the sparse head of a real but thin distribution. Unpublished paying structure exists in the exposed class at roughly one-per-sixty-models density, and it arrives via a novel spelled op-tuple the shipped grammar cannot express (mul(unsqueeze, unsqueeze) from a delta-rule recurrence), not via recombination of structure the corpus already shares. The dominant discovery mode observed at scale is re-derivation: the proposal families re-find banked laws where purpose-built probes supply the pattern, and the honest gates correctly stop conditional truth short of shipping.
  • For the thesis: if unpublished paying rewrites are the yield, the exposed class's marginal density is ~1.5% per model — the corpus needs either many more novel spelled architectures (DeltaRuleBlock's class: real recurrent math spelled elementwise, not fused-op natives and not synthetic probes) or the guarded-law machinery to close on the conditional leads (mul_unsqueeze_r_id fires on 12 real models — the largest measured paying structure still stuck behind unsq:d_in_pad).

Tooling: lab/screen/discovery_scale.py (the budgeted sweep + corpus novelty driver), lab/screen/exposed_discovery.py (+source:name qualified selection, lhs_data/rhs_data serialization in evidence rows). Evidence: project/retros/discovery-scale.json (untracked).
