kit-term-bridge — mlopt terms emitted as .hnl, evaluated through CatoptKit ​

Date: 2026-10-20 · Surface: packages/mlopt-kit/src/bin/hnl-run.rs (+ tests/cli.rs), lab/kit/term_hnl.py, tests/test_kit_bridge.py · Gates: cargo test -p mlopt-kit — 21/21 green; pytest tests/test_kit_bridge.py — 8/8 green (marked e2e: the suite crosses the external engine boundary).

What landed ​

The frontier after mlopt-kit (retro mlopt-kit.md) was the second half of "the e-graph's terms and the kit's runtime are the same program space": mlopt's optimization machinery emitting/consuming programs through the kit, not just hand-written .hnl. Three pieces, each thin:

  • hnl-run — a subprocess door over CatoptKit: .hnl in on stdin or a file path, the serde_json::Value boundary form out ({"__tensor__": …} for a tensor). --project <dir> wires a .handl.toml; --repeat N evaluates the source N times inside one kit — the measurement hook a costed decision needs ((t(N) − t(1))/(N−1) wall per call). Deliberately program-generic — nothing term-aware — so emitted terms and the hnl/ modules go through the same door.
  • lab/kit/term_hnl.py — the emit/eval bridge. emit renders any term as a tensor.* expression from a data table (_OpSpec per op: slots assemble the call; accepts pins attrs the surface can honour — conv2d has no dilation slot, batch_norm has no training=True; drop ignores the eval-inert momentum/ cudnn_enabled; arity picks the signature by operand count — batch_norm's export variants synthesize the identity affine the op requires). emit_program binds Var/Param leaves in a do- prelude: {"shape","data"} → reshape(from_list(flat)), {"hnl"} → a verbatim expression (the marshaling channel for computed inputs — the fold's s). KitRunner shells hnl-run and decodes the wire form.
  • tests/test_kit_bridge.py — the verification, three claims.

Verified ​

  1. A real artifact end to end. TorchSource().to_ir of an eval-mode Conv2d → BatchNorm2d — the actual aten.conv2d + aten.batch_norm IR with its full attr tail — emits as tensor.batch_norm(tensor.conv2d(x, p_conv_weight, p_conv_bias, [1,1], [1,1]), …, 0.00001) and evaluates through the kit to the torch-fp64 answer (max abs diff 6.7e-16 on a seeded fixture).
  2. A law's two forms through the runtime. conv_channel_scale (the CONV_FOLD_RULES anchor) applied via meta.apply_rewrite_at: mul(conv2d(x,W,b), sv) and its conv2d(x, W∘sv′, b∘sv) rewrite both emit and evaluate dyadic-exact-equal — and dag_cost(flops_cost) credits the fold (the absorbed mul/reshape are param-only subtrees — compile-time work).
  3. An optimization decision costed through the runtime. The conv+BN fold as a decision: spelled batch_norm(conv2d(x,W,b), g, be, m, v, eps) vs deployed conv2d(x, W2, b2) — where W2/b2 are mlopt subterms (mul(w, reshape(s,(C,1,1,1))), add(mul(b,s), sub(be,mul(s,m)))) materialized through the kit itself. Deployed == unfolded (< 1e-12), and the measured per-call cost is 82.1 ms vs 95.4 ms (--repeat 30, debug build) with dag_cost agreeing directionally (36864 vs 55296). The fold's value lands where it should — in the deployed program.
  4. Generality + the honest bound. A matmul/add/sub/reshape/transpose/concat term evaluates against the torch reference; pack emits a multi-output list; sigmoid, rsqrt, and training-mode batch_norm each refuse by name.

The precisely-named missing surface ​

The one piece of the conv+BN fold the bridge cannot emit as a term: s = γ·rsqrt(σ²+ε) — elementwise unary ops are absent from host-tensor's 18-op surface (rsqrt/sqrt/neg/exp/sigmoid, and the reductions sum/mean/max). The spelled RHS of the fold composite dies on rsqrt first (UnsupportedOpError("rsqrt"), tested). Workaround exercised here: s rides the {"hnl"} marshaling channel — the (C,) stats materialize to lists and scalar ** finishes each channel, hnl/conv_bn.hnl's own escape. The honest fix is a host-tensor op addition (elementwise unaries), not bridge machinery.

Findings worth carrying ​

  • The handl lexer does not read scientific notation. 1e-05 lexes as the token sequence 1 e -05 — silently evaluating to 1 in statement position (measured: e :: 1e-05 bound e = 1), and failing inside a list. A mlopt eps=1e-05 emitted naively would silently become eps=1. _num expands every float through Decimal (1e-05 → 0.00001), and the emitted-source test pins the spelling. This is an upstream lexer gap worth a handl-side fix.
  • dag_cost's param-only discount models compile-time folding the kit runtime does not perform — the emitted program evaluates mul(w, reshape(sv)) per call. That is why the fold-decision test separates compute (the fold, run once) from deploy (folded weights as leaf data): the per-call measurement then prices what the cost model prices. Reported honestly: through the bridge, an unfolded mul(conv,sv) is cheaper per call than its folded RHS spelled as runtime work.
  • Bound names: torch-export leaf names are dotted (conv.weight) and dots are field access in handl — _sanitize maps to conv_weight; tensor itself is reserved (binding it would shadow the op namespace).
  • The upstream hnl CLI (host-cli) is not a usable door: it panics in plugin-engine-typecheck-handl on a trivial program and, more fundamentally, has no host-tensor OpRegistry linked — the tensor.* ops are Rust impls only CatoptKit wires. That is why hnl-run lives in mlopt-kit.

What remains open (named) ​

  • The e-graph → .hnl loop is one direction deep. The bridge emits terms into the kit; nothing yet lifts a TaggedValue::Tensor or an .hnl program back into IR (the OnnxSource/OnnxSink precedent says the seam shape is a Source/Sink pair — a KitSink implementing ports.Sink.lower/verify is the natural next step).
  • Promotion floor. lab/kit/term_hnl.py sits in lab/ under the 99%-coverage promotion rule; its tests are e2e-marked, so the dev lane (-m "not slow") runs them but -m "not e2e" does not.
  • --repeat timing is wall-clock in a debug build — directionally honest, not a benchmark rig.
