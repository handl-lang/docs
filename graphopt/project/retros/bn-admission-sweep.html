Retro — BATCH_NORM_RULES admission sweep: truth green, pay refuses ​

Object: the three eval-mode expansion laws (batch_norm_expand, batch_norm_expand_nobias, batch_norm_expand_plain, mlopt_core/laws/tensor.py), held out of DEFAULT pending the guarded sweep — the same gauntlet the promoted laws cleared (admission-gauntlet.md, mul-unsq-nat.md).

Verdict: NOT admitted. The sweep ran green through stage 4 (truth) and refused at stage 6 (typed-pay). DEFAULT unchanged — the sweep is the floor and it refused.

How it ran ​

The gauntlet operates on stored objects, so each rule was stored verbatim (store_object, kind="law", cert=None — all three serialize fully: missing_hooks empty, serializable: True) into a scratch store mlopt_discovery.cache_dir()/bn_admission.db, then run_gauntlet(key, corpus=default_gauntlet_corpus()). The real corpus assembled in 10.1 s (364 real terms / 245 probe cases / 83 base rules); each gauntlet ran 0.6–9.9 s — the sweep is cheap on this box, no bounding needed.

Why the refusal is the honest one ​

The truth gate is exactly what the family needed to clear: every guard-accepted evaluable binding is equal (16/16 synthesized, 10/10 real for the full variant; the two short arities accept nothing real because aten exports always carry the affine operands — their guarded region is corpus-empty but the sweep is not vacuous: 16 equal synth sites each).

The refusal is typed-pay (evd.paid > 0 required): the expand direction writes the fused kernel's spelled work into the e-graph — a member that is never the cheapest (batch_norm prices 4 vs the ~7-pass spelled subgraph, tensor.py's own header comment). On the 8 corpus sites the full variant fires, extraction never selects the member — paid=0. nobias/plain match no corpus arity at all (fires=0).

An expose/infrastructure law that cannot win extraction anywhere is not a DEFAULT candidate under this floor — the promoted laws each paid ≥1 site. Its value is enabling: it gives downstream weight-space laws the spelled per-channel scale/shift to see (conv-bn-fold-synth.md's anchor story). Admission routes that recognize enablement rather than extraction pay would be a different, unbuilt floor — recording the gate's verdict, not working around it.

What stays open ​

  • BATCH_NORM_RULES remains opt-in (search(..., rules=DEFAULT + BATCH_NORM_RULES)); re-run the gauntlet if a corpus grows batch_norm-free export arities or an enablement-aware admission floor is built.
  • The batch_norm_expand matches (10) live in the corpus — the real BN sites exist (ResNetBlock et al.), so the family's truth was measured on real instances, not only synthesized ones.
