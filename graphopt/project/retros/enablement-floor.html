Retro — cached fixpoint + the enablement floor, measured ​

Follows: fixpoint-api-and-conv-sweep.md (its two open items). Commits: 02e05f9 (rulecache knobs + payload-internal parents), ca42c16 (guard transport for stored composites), this commit (probe + retro + GOALS).

Item 1 — synthesize_rules_cached takes the fixpoint API ​

rounds / max_emitted / on_round now pass through synthesize_rules_cached into meta.synthesize_rules, and both scalar knobs join cache_key's params — a rounds=3 mint is a different problem instance from rounds=1 and must not collide. on_round is a callable: it enters the key through its _hook_sig proxy (source text when inspectable, else module+qualname) — a vetoing observer truncates the fixpoint, so sharing the unobserved entry would be a wrong hit; an uninspectable signature degrades to a miss. Documented hit semantics: the observer reports live rounds only — a warm load calls nothing.

Also landed, from the same retro's "unbuilt" list: RuleCache.load resolves parents payload-internally — records decode in stored order and each decoded rule joins the resolver, so a round-≥2 composite whose parent is itself a syn_* mint round-trips self-contained (emission order guarantees parents precede dependents; all-or-nothing miss semantics unchanged).

Tests (tests/test_rulecache.py §e, 4 tests): cached rounds=3 returns the fixpoint strata under a monkeypatched-exploding synthesizer; rounds=1/rounds=3/max_emitted=2 are distinct entries; the observer fires on cold mints only and a vetoing callable keys separately; a guarded syn_*-parented composite reloads its guard from the payload.

Item 2 — the enablement floor, measured ​

The named blocker is fixed: guard transport for stored composites. store_object writes the minted rule's guard_pats + parents as a "guard" record field (the rulecache binding wire form — additive; older readers ignore it); admit_object resolves the named parents as rows of the same store transitively and re-runs meta._compose_guards. missing_hooks/serializable keep their meaning (what the bare record rebuilds); admit annotates the record with "transported", and full-data counts missing-minus-transported. A parent that rebuilt weaker than it declared refuses the transport — composing over it would smuggle a weakened guard. 5 tests in tests/test_discovery_gauntlet.py cover spec-write, restore+fire, transitivity, the honest partial admit, and the gate.

The premise-pay measurement (lab/probes/conv_fold_premise_pay_probe.py) ​

The conv+BN fold composite mints in 4 rounds (974 emitted), premise closure = 8 rules (batch_norm_expand + 4 conv_channel_* anchors + 3 syn intermediates). Stored, admitted — transported=['check','derive'] — and measured on the real eval-mode ConvBN site:

stagemeasured
transporthooks rebuilt transitively; composite fires on the seed

payfires=1, typed=1, ill=0, verified=same — and changed=False: extraction kept the spelled term (spelled=34874.3 vs fused=34874.337)

The dependent mint does not pay in extraction. The composite's fused member exists in the e-class but dag_cost prices its RHS's spelled weight-fold trees (mul/reshape/rsqrt/add over the bound params) as real ops — the constant-fold win materializes only at lowering (IRModule._fold_weight_chains), a layer the term-level cost model cannot see. The premise-of-admitted-composite accounting is now implemented and ran; the pay leg is measured false, not assumed.

Caveat on the formal floor: the full run_gauntlet on the composite did not terminate in pl.measure on this box (>9 min even on a one-case corpus — the 83-rule _reach/derivability saturation is a scaling gap, separate from starvation). The per-stage numbers above are the bounded measurement.

Verdicts per family ​

  • conv anchors in the closure (conv_channel_shift_sub_sp, conv_channel_scale_sp, conv_channel_scale_l_sp, conv_channel_shift_sp) + batch_norm_expand: enabled-but-opt-in — a dependent mint exists, fires, and truth-verifies (real region + premise-anchored replayable cert), but pays nothing in extraction → the floor's pay leg fails → not DEFAULT. Enablement (the mint exists at all) is real; pay is the measured refusal.
  • the other 56 CONV_FOLD_RULES: named refusal — truth-starved AND no paying dependent mint measured.
  • the composite itself: now storable/admittable via transport and premise-derivable, but does not pay → not a DEFAULT candidate.
  • BATCH_NORM_RULES (the other arities): unchanged — fires=0 variants have no dependent mint; batch_norm_expand shares the closure's account.

What stays open ​

  • The corner-reaching truth route (enumeration vocabulary for conv2d/channel-views, or witness-driven sites) — requirement #1 still stands; the premise-anchored verify_law replay is the honest partial substitute measured here.
  • run_gauntlet/pl.measure on guarded minted composites does not terminate within the box — the reach/derivability saturation needs a bounded budget for composite-scale rules.
  • A cost model that credits compile-time weight folds (the fused member's spelled weight trees are free after _fold_weight_chains) would flip the measured pay leg — that is a cost-model gap, not a law-truth gap.

DEFAULT unchanged. Gate account: ruff/format/ty clean on touched files; tests/test_rulecache{,_codec}.py, test_meta_fixpoint.py, tests/test_discovery_{gauntlet,evidence}.py all pass (34 + 58 + 44; the standalone test_rulecache fp64 tests still need the suite's torch-import order — pre-existing at HEAD, verified).
