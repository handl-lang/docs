Retro — the postnorm family: log_softmax's wrapped-reduction gap closed ​

Scope: mlopt_discovery/handler_synth.py, lab/audit/grammar_probe.py, tests/test_discovery_handler_synth.py.

Depends on: project/retros/grammar-gaps.md (the residue this closes), ADR 0007 layer 2 (the handler grammar), plan 0025 §B (grammar extension by measured refusal, not taste).

The residue ​

grammar-gaps.md named it: "log_softmax (a real invisible corpus op, 2 sites) — a normalization whose reduction result is wrapped in a unary (log . sum . exp) or a logsumexp reduction; the norm template covers a bare reduction operand only, and logsumexp has no torch binding." A norm body computes op(lhs, red(rhs, axis, keepdim)) — the reduction output is a bare operand of the outer op. log_softmax(x) = x - log(sum(exp x)) needs the reduction result wrapped in log, so no norm body verifies — a grammar-coverage refusal (candidates proposed, none verify).

The extension ​

("postnorm", op, lhs, post, red_op, rhs, axis, keepdim) — op(lhs, post(red_op(rhs, axis, keepdim))), where post is a unary from the new POST_UNARY vocabulary. Deliberately minimal:

  • POST_UNARY = ("log",) — only the measured wrap. Other wraps are a grammar extension, not a candidate.
  • The family is seed-only: _canonical_postnorm_seeds proposes op(meta, post(red_op(ew, axis, keepdim))) — a bare-meta lhs (the value being centered/scaled) over the shallow elementwise rhs vocabulary, op/post/red_op/axis over their banks. ~160 candidates for a 1-meta spec. No full column walk exists — a non-canonical postnorm body is a declared grammar gap, not hidden search.
  • Same shape gate as norm (proposed only when the spec's top op is in NORM_OPS); _admitted_families admits it for any non-empty meta set; _GRAMMAR_VERSION bumps to handler-grammar-v2 (the vocabulary changed, so the provenance stamp must too — one existing pin updated).
  • Records round-trip unchanged: body_to_data/handler_from_record are generic over the descriptor tuple, and provenance.family reads body[0] = "postnorm".

Measured ​

  • log-softmax spec (sub(X, log(sum(exp X, -1, keepdim)))) — minted ("postnorm", "sub", "X", "log", "sum", ("exp","X"), -1, True); verified at the fp64 gate, and the descriptor agrees with torch.nn.functional.log_softmax to 1e-12 (pinned by test).
  • grammar_probe.py battery gains the "log-softmax" spec so the census reports the family's reach.
  • The other grammar-gap residue — the stabilized (clamped) scan — stays open: it is a non-affine two-state recurrence, a semantics extension, not a vocabulary one (unchanged verdict).

Gates ​

  • pytest tests/test_discovery_handler_synth.py — 47 passed (4 new: eval_body on the descriptor, the log_softmax mint, the shape gate, the record round-trip).
  • ruff check / ruff format --check on the touched files — clean.
