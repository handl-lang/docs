ADR 0008 — Combinator presentation of the IR (monoidal + trace) ​

Depends on: ADR 0004 (abstraction as move — minted atoms become definable under this presentation), ADR 0005 (the open game), ADR 0007 (semantic-atom minting — the runtime-assembly loop runs over combinator terms). Related: pack (already the proto-monoidal product), the carrier atoms (om_decayed, applyd, decay_vec — hand-minted; this ADR makes them definable), the sharing-ambiguity scars (DAG-vs-tree semantics under e-graph congruence). Literature basis: the categorical calculus for neural architectures (Gavranović, Lessard, McCourt, de Jong van Lier, Sanada et al., "Categorical Deep Learning") — architectures as morphisms of a monoidal category, parameters via Param, gradients via lenses — plus the arrow/loop line (Hughes) for first-class recurrence.

Context ​

The recurring user question this week — "why is X hardcoded?" — has a single root: the object language is an op-graph, so anything that isn't an op-node needs a hand-minted atom. Carrier atoms (om_decayed, applyd, decay_vec, aff_diag) are authored primitives; multi-output needed a bespoke pack; sharing is implicit in the DAG and has bitten us (the inf-norm sharing divergence); weight-space rewrites need a new law family because parameters are leaves, not morphisms. ADR-0007 retires part of this by minting atoms mechanically — but minted atoms are still atoms: the object language has no combinator structure of its own.

The categorical presentation offers the minimal universal grammar: objects are types, morphisms are terms, and a small combinator basis — composition >>>, monoidal product ⊗, structural morphisms (id, swap, assoc, unitors), diagonal Δ for sharing — presents every computation graph. Two extensions cover the neural specifics:

  • Param morphisms (CDL): a weight is a first-class component of the morphism, not an input leaf — conv+BN folding becomes an ordinary equation between morphism expressions, no new law family.
  • loop/trace (Hughes): recurrence is first-class — a scan is loop (arr step), and the carrier atoms become spellings of the trace construction rather than primitives.

Decision ​

Add a combinator presentation layer over the IR — parallel, not a replacement. The op-graph IR stays the lowering surface (sinks, executors, pack); the combinator presentation is the discovery and semantics surface.

The presentation ​

  • Terms are morphisms of a symmetric monoidal category with Δ (sharing is a morphism, not a pointer convention) plus loop (typed trace — state shape fixed).
  • A bridge, both directions: dag → combinator (fan-out becomes explicit Δ; pack becomes ⊗) and combinator → dag (lower via expansion of structure morphisms).
  • Laws remain equations — now between combinator expressions. The same admission gauntlet applies (ADR 0004/0007): oracle → typed gate → certificate → adversarial sweep → measured pay.

What becomes definable (the payoff) ​

  • Carrier atoms as loop instances. applyd/om_decayed/ decay_vec cease to be primitives — the RetNet carrier is a spelled trace term. The authored-atom problem dissolves.
  • Weight-space laws as ordinary rewrites. Params as morphisms → conv+BN folding, reparameterization, linear-chain folding are equations in the same object language as everything else.
  • Sharing as structure. Δ makes fan-out explicit — the DAG-vs-tree ambiguity class of bugs is impossible by construction.
  • A uniform discovery space. Proposers enumerate morphism shapes of the free category — naturality/factorization families stop being separately-coded generators.

What this does NOT buy — stated so we don't overclaim ​

  • Search gets uniform, not smaller. A universal substrate enumerates an enormous space; proposer/guidance machinery remains the bottleneck. The menus stop being authored op-patterns but guidance (which morphism families to enumerate, budget policy, measured need) is still a designed component.
  • loop has real typing subtleties. Trace requires a fixed state shape; dynamically-shaped recurrence is out of scope initially (constant shape per term is the checkable contract).
  • Verification machinery is unchanged. The oracle/cert/sweep gauntlet is representation-agnostic; the presentation changes what terms look like, not what "sound" means.

Falsification path ​

The direction fails, measurably, if:

  1. The bridge loses structure. dag → combinator → dag does not round-trip on the corpus (survivors verified end-to-end), or the Δ-expansion blows term size past the enode budget on representative exports.
  2. Nothing authored becomes definable. If applyd/om_decayed cannot be respelled as trace terms within the basis (measured: a corpus workload saturates to the carrier through loop terms), the claimed payoff is empty.
  3. The combinator space is unusably large for discovery. If enumerating morphism shapes to depth ~6 yields nothing firing that the op-pattern enumerators already find cheaper, uniformity bought us elegance, not reach.
  4. Two-way drift. If maintaining the dual presentation forces semantic fixes to land twice or diverge, the parallel layer is worse than the problem.

Honest scars carried forward ​

  • Authoring moves, not disappears. The combinator basis itself, the Δ/loop law sets, and the bridge are authored — same bootstrapping disclaimer as the law bank. The claim is that the authored surface gets smaller and more uniform, not that it vanishes.
  • An authored thing that retires must retire measured. Carrier atoms remain until a corpus workload reaches the carrier via loop terms end-to-end — parity-or-better, verified. Until then both spellings coexist (provenance-marked).
  • The sharing fix is the strongest immediate argument. If Δ only eliminates one historical bug class and nothing else follows, that alone may not justify the layer.
  • Representation upgrades have a graveyard. The prior exposure screens keep paying because they measured; this ADR's value is bounded by its falsification checks running, not by the elegance of the substrate.

Consequences ​

  • The authored atom set shrinks to the combinator basis — the smallest hand-written surface the system can have while remaining an object language at all.
  • Discovery targets morphisms, not op-tuples — proposer grammars become "which combinator shapes," strictly more uniform.
  • A runtime is a diagram family. The ADR-0007 emergent-runtime loop mints combinator terms; a generated package = {basis extensions, laws, verified handlers} all in one representation.
  • Both surfaces coexist deliberately — the op-graph stays for lowering (sinks already speak it); the combinator layer is where semantics, laws, and discovery live. The bridge is the seam.

Measurements (added post-landing) ​

Bridge prototype + falsification gates measured (841b99c, 481eec7, merged 32962d0; lab/bridge/combinator.py + lab/bridge/combinator_probe.py).

  • Gate 1 — corpus round-trip: PASS. 321/321 exported workloads (zoo + intake + model registries) round-trip structurally exact (hash-consed identity); the 2 rejections are upstream torch.export failures, not bridge gaps. Mean blowup 2.61× (max 7.27× on a sharing-heavy case); largest morphism 1.1% of the enode budget. Zero ops refuse translation. 10 sampled survivors lower through TorchSink bit-identical.

  • Gate 2 — carrier as a loop term: PASS. The shared-state fold spells as seq(pre, loop(step, K), post), lowered and verified against zoo._ChunkedRetention at 1e-7–2.6e-7 (the documented γ⁻ᶜ conditioning bound). Named extension: bounded scanloop_n — a bare trace Tr^S is insufficient; n-fold iteration over block inputs is the required combinator. Bonus: the staged-saturation extract projects through the bridge round-trip EQUAL (1590-node morphism, 92 explicit Δ sites) — same recurrence modulo spelling.

  • Gate 3 — MEASURED 2026-10-15: PARTIAL, and the partial is the useful part. Ran the protocol (--gate2 --extracted; staged arm T=64 K=4, 15 s, 9,734 enodes):

    extracted term: dag=139  reads=2 chained=True
projects: round-trip EQUAL  morph=1590 dup=92 copies=184
fold-chain depth: spelled=3  extract=2

    Read against the criterion: the search does reach the algorithmic content — a chained shared-state fold (chained=True, depth 2 against the spelled 3) — and that term projects through the presentation EQUAL. What it does not produce is a loop-headed term, and the probe says why in its own words:

    the DAG bridge never mints loop — it is a proposed form, not a recovered one.

    So gate 3 splits, and the split is actionable:

    • as an expression — PASS: the calculus spells recurrences that lower and verify (gate 2), and the search's winner round-trips through it.
    • as a search space — NOT YET: a presentation that can only project an extracted term cannot be reached by search. Closing gate 3 needs a proposing rule — a rewrite whose right-hand side introduces loop — so the question becomes whether saturation selects it, which is a different (and now well-posed) experiment.

    What this means for ADR 0009 stage 4: a catopt-kernel is justified as the home of rules over combinators (the proposing rule is exactly such a rule), not as a projection target. The stage-4 framing should say that, and this measurement is why. (Measured 2026-10-16 — the proposing rule was built; it works, but the search does not reach it. See the FAIL bullet immediately below, which supersedes this paragraph's optimistic reading of stage 4.)

  • Gate 3 — MEASURED 2026-10-16: FAIL. The proposing rule works; the search does not reach it. Built the rule the PARTIAL bullet above asked for — lab/bridge/loop_propose.py (commit b7ba9ff): a rewrite unroll(step, n) → Loop(step, n) over the presentation, plus the smallest search that can carry it (a term-level rewrite closure with cost extraction). unroll(step, n) is the presentation's spelled bounded scan — one shared step, explicit perms — and the tests prove it denotes the same morphism as Loop(step, n) (comb_to_dag(unroll(step,n)) == comb_to_dag(Loop(step,n))). The gate-3 command is unchanged, now with the rule armed:

    sh
    .venv/bin/python lab/bridge/combinator_probe.py --gate2 --extracted --propose-loop
    extracted term: dag=139  reads=2 chained=True
projects: round-trip EQUAL  morph=1590 dup=92 copies=184
fold-chain depth: spelled=3  extract=2
the presentation's spelled scan is in the search's root e-class: False
-- gate 3: the `loop`-proposing rule --
   loop-propose: winner morph=1590 spelled-scan subterms (n>=2) found=0 ns=[]
      saturation: terms=1 fired={'loop_propose': 0, 'loop_expand': 0}
        loop-headed reachable=False  best_is_loop=False  best_cost=1590
   control (authored spelled scan): spelled-scan subterms=1 ns=[4]
      fired={'loop_propose': 1, 'loop_expand': 0}  loop-headed reachable=True
      best_is_loop=True  best_cost=69 (spelled 285)

    Two measurements, both negative for the gate:

    1. The rule is well-formed. On the authored spelled scan (unroll(retnet_step, 4)) it fires once and the closure's cheapest term is Loop-headed (cost 69 against the spelled 285). That is the control — it proves the rewrite itself works, not that the search reaches it.
    2. The search never reaches it. The winner's projection is a flat DAG compilation (morph=1590) with zerounroll(step, n≥2) subterms, so the rule fires 0× and the search stays un-loop-ed. Independently, the presentation's spelled scan lands in a different e-class from the root (False) — the saturation never derives it, so no rule keyed on that spelling could ever fire.

    Why (measured, and deeper than "the rule was too narrow"): the export's unrolled recurrence is parametrically regular but not syntactically regular. Each block's step differs by the block index (split(…, index=i)) and the reassociation re-spells the decay rate, so no fixed syntactic pattern — and in particular no presentation unroll(step, n), whose whole point is a single shared step — matches it. Reaching the loop therefore needs abstraction over the step (a form-level / parametric move), not an e-graph rewrite. The presentation makes the loop expressible (gate 2, PASS) but does not by itself make it findable.

    What this means for ADR 0009 stage 4: the gate measured negative, so on this evidence a catopt-kernel is not justified as a discovery substrate — the presentation is a presentation, not a search space. Stage 4 now rests on something else or on nothing; this is exactly the outcome ADR 0009's own falsification clause asked to be measured and written down ("if search cannot reach loop-headed forms, stage 4 is not justified"). A real combinator search — a form-level proposer that abstracts the step — is the missing piece, and it is a different and larger piece of work than "promote the prototype to a package".

  • Gate 3 — MEASURED 2026-10-16 (later): still FAIL — the parametric-abstraction move was built, and it names a deeper cause. The FAIL bullet above named the missing piece ("abstraction over the step"); this is that move, built and measured — lab/bridge/index_abstract.py (commit e4a193a), wired as --abstract-index. It binds the per-block index (and the recurrence state) as parameters (a parameter is a wire — no new node kind), collapses the K blocks to one shared step when the family is regular, and emits unroll(step, K) for the proposing rule to fold. The gate-3 command is unchanged, both arms armed:

      family sizes/dim=('(16, 16, 16, 16)', -2) indices=[0, 1, 2, 3]
      family sizes/dim=('(4, 4, 4, 4, 16, 16, 16)', -2) indices=[0, 1, 2, 3]
      verdict=IRREGULAR — the 7 blocks do NOT collapse to one shared step
      first difference: block1/block2/add[1]/mul[1]: op om_decayed vs add
   control (regular fold): blocks=4 verdict=regular round-trip=True  closure: best_is_loop=True best_cost=109

    The control is the presentation's own spelled fold, expanded (the gate-2 program): the move collapses it to one shared step, round-trips, and the closure reaches a Loop (best_is_loop=True) — measured at K = 2, 4, 8. So the move works; the winner still does not collapse. The index was not the whole story: the winner's output is a concat of 7 blocks of two sizes (4×size-4 + 3×size-16, from two split families), and extraction mixed two state spellings — the shared-state read matmul(q, S) and the intra re-expansion om_decayed(matmul(q, k_prevᵀ), D, v_prev). Canonicalising every block modulo the varying integers (index, t, offset, sign) leaves 7 distinct blocks, so no constant-parameter abstraction can collapse them. Removing the index is necessary but not sufficient; the remaining gap is the state spelling and the chunking.

    What this means for ADR 0009 stage 4: unchanged and strengthened — the gate is negative and the missing piece is larger than the previous bullet implied (a move that normalises the state spelling and the chunking, not just a form-level proposer). See project/retros/gate3-parametric-abstraction.md.

  • Gate 3 — MEASURED 2026-10-16 (later still): still FAIL — cause 2 (the mixed state spelling) is an extraction tie, and the fix is real but does not close the gate. The parametric-abstraction bullet above named "extraction mixed two state spellings" without measuring it; this is that measurement plus the principled fix — lab/probes/gate3_state_spelling_probe.py and lab/bridge/sharing_extract.py (commit aa56e55), wired as --sharing-extract. Same staged arm (T=64 K=4, 9733 9,784 enodes, PYTHONHASHSEED=0); the winner is unchanged (dag=139, 455,752 FLOPs, morph=1590, round-trip EQUAL) and its read sequence is reexp|reexp|fold|fold|reexp|shared|shared.

    The mechanism, measured: 5 of the 7 state-read e-classes have ≥ 2 members at the class minimum (2-way in blocks 0–3, a 4-way tie at 232,000 in block 4), so extract_best's tie-break — nops, then member sort order (op, children, repr(attrs)), i.e. op name — decides the spelling, arbitrarily with respect to sharing. Forcing the factored matmul(q, S) read per class moves the whole term by Δ ∈ {−528, 0, +16, +16, +144} FLOPs: the model is indifferent (±0.03 %), and in block 4 it strictly prefers the factored read (which shares its state with block 5's read) — a non-local saving the greedy pass cannot see. So the greedy extraction is suboptimal:

    greedy           cost 455,752   dag=139   morph=1590   round-trip EQUAL
sharing-aware    cost 451,128   (was 432,696)         round-trip EQUAL
                 Δ = -4,624 (-1.01 %, was -23,056 / -5.06 %),
                 max|greedy - fix| = 2.220e-16

    The fix — extract_sharing_aware: a bounded, monotone coordinate descent (force one class at a time; keep only strict improvements to the whole term's dag_cost) — removes the arbitrary choice and adds the real sharing signal. It is numerically verified equivalent to machine epsilon, and it still leaves the gate FAIL: the abstraction's verdict is IRREGULAR with the same first difference (block1/block2/add[1]/mul[1]: op om_decayed vs add). The mix is a cascade of per-class ties, and the small-chunk family (C=4 < d=16) genuinely prefers the unfactored spelling (C²d < Cd²) — so forcing it would be manufacturing a result, not a fix. The remaining causes are the two chunk widths (the abstraction abstracts index/t/offset/sign but not sizes) and the abstraction's own structural state identification (per family it binds state_in = None|unsqueeze|unsqueeze|unsqueeze for the small family and None|matmul|mul for the large one).

    What this means for ADR 0009 stage 4: unchanged. The gate is still negative, and this task retires one candidate fix: the state spelling cannot be normalised by a principled tie-break, because part of the mix is a genuine cost optimum. See project/retros/gate3-extraction-state-spelling.md. (Correction 2026-10-15, abf6114: the recovery above is 451,128 (−1.01 %, 3 overrides), not 432,696 (−5.06 %, 5 overrides) — 432,696 was a hash-order artifact of the pre-fix scan, and the read sequence is unchanged from greedy. The caveat this bullet used to carry — the staged saturation was not reproducible across processes (9733/9752/9756 enodes) — is resolved: abf6114 made the saturation and the sharing-aware optimum deterministic (canonical member order), reproducible in every process under PYTHONHASHSEED 0–6 + unset. Numbers reported before that fix may be seeded/arbitrary. The defect is real but 1.01 %, a much weaker case for promotion.)

    The measurement: abstracting sizes (and index/t/offset/sign) leaves 7 distinct templates — on the greedy winner and on the sharing-aware one alike — so the width was never the binding constraint.   • Gate 3 — the protocol (added 2026-10-14; it is the last gate standing between this ADR and ADR 0009's stage 4, so "open" without a way to close it was the real problem). loop structure is erased at export: fused recurrences (lstm.input, cumsum) translate fine but the fold must be proposed by search, never recovered by the bridge. Whether search reaches loop forms is the remaining substrate risk.

    The protocol (one command, one criterion):

    sh
    .venv/bin/python lab/bridge/combinator_probe.py --gate2 --extracted

    This runs one bounded staged saturation (om_shared_state_stages) over the exported RetNet graph and projects the extracted winner through the bridge. Read the result as:

    • PASS — the extracted winner is loop-headed (or contains a loop at the shared-state position), and it round-trips through the bridge equal. Then the presentation is a discovery substrate: the search reached a form the export erased, and ADR 0009's stage 4 is justified on this evidence.
    • FAIL — the winner is the spelled (unrolled) form again. Then search did not reach loop, and the honest reading is that the bridge is a presentation (a way to say recurrences) rather than a search space, and stage 4 should not be justified by it.

    Why this is the right criterion: gate 2 already proves the spelling works (a hand-written loop term lowers and verifies at 1e-7). Gate 3 is the only question a presentation cannot answer about itself — whether the search, not the author, gets there. A negative result is therefore as valuable as a positive one, and both are publishable statements about the substrate.

    Prior evidence, for calibration (from the staged-saturation run recorded above): the extract did reach the carrier at t512 (TestRealExportFold::test_extraction_prefers_the_chunked_carrier_at_t512) and the staged extract round-tripped EQUAL with 92 explicit Δ sites — which is encouraging but not the gate: those wins are carrier-rooted extractions, not a loop-headed winner at the shared-state position. The protocol above is what distinguishes them.

  • Cost scars. Permutation bookkeeping dominates morphism mass (Σperm 6057 > Σdag 3519) — the flat context encoding is honest but wasteful; a real layer wants a better normal form. Also: the shared-state probe's _honest_reference_cost fold is numerically off (missing /√T on cross terms, a γ^C on ξ) — valid as a FLOP floor (attrs don't move FLOPs), but it was never a verified program; the spelled loop is.
