ADR 0013 — the stop decision: an eight-round characterization ​

Status: measured, open. Series: results_stop_policy.md (r1–2) → results_stop_advantage.md (r6, self-posterior + actor-critic) → results_stop_valuelabel.md (r7, expected-yield labels) → results_stop_calibrated.md (r8, Platt/isotonic calibration) → results_stop_isolated.md (r9, the delivery outside the softmax — GatedPlayer + the forced_action engine hook).

The problem ​

Should the open-game player keep evaluating pending candidates or stop? The oracle ceiling is measured: +0.026 reward/ep and 4.3× yield-per-eval (3.75 evals vs the incumbent's ~15) — real, and unreachable so far.

What eight rounds eliminated ​

blockerroundverdict
featuresr1–2shared-softmax stop logit drowns; supervised head degenerates to never-stop (Bayes-rational under asymmetric costs)
creditr6per-step advantage (actor-critic AdvantagePlayer) rescues AP vs collapse — real mechanism gain
observabilityr6policy's own posterior (open:self) ≈0.33 solo AP vs oracle labels — signal exists at zero predictor cost
value labelsr7open:vlabel expected-yield columns real, but predictors miscalibrate in magnitude held-out
calibrationr8Platt/isotonic fixes magnitude (log-loss 3–5×, ECE ≤0.05) — best headless gate: isotonic useful-pool 0.2929 @ 12.8 evals, 26 real stops
deliveryr9the isolated channel (forced_action + GatedPlayer) clears both bars honestly — iso-head:isotonic 0.2892/AP 0.2101 @ 26 real stops, seam-free; the fold twin never-stops. Shared-softmax hypothesis confirmed for delivery — residual is evidence (all clears are tail-trims)

The residual blocker (stated exactly) ​

At calibrated probabilities the break-even rule rationally drains (cost asymmetry: missed useful −1.0 vs wasted eval −0.002). No serializable arm clears both bake bars (reward ≥0.273 ∧ AP ≥0.210). What remains missing is held-out discrimination — a predictor that ranks pending candidates above chance out-of-distribution — or a stop delivery outside the shared softmax (the recurring corruption channel: five feature families all collapse AP the same way).

Transfer ​

The lesson generalizes beyond stop: any gated decision in the game needs (a) features that see the decision-relevant quantity, (b) credit assigned per-step, (c) calibrated magnitudes, (d) a delivery channel isolated from ranking logits. Rounds 1–8 are the measured map of which of those was actually the wall at each stage.
