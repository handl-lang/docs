ADR 0011 — which representation is canonical: the calculus for laws, the op-graph for execution ​

Depends on: ADR 0002 (categorical re-expression), ADR 0003 (four dimensions), ADR 0008 (combinator presentation — and its two-way-drift warning), ADR 0009 (the ladder; stage 3 names this decision), ADR 0010 (one language, many interpretations). Informed by: spike Q0 (catopt's vocabulary assembles as handl data — PASS, handl b20b978a, catopt 8d5b47d) and spike Q1a (a tensor crosses by reference as a ForeignResource).

Context ​

Two representations of the same programs coexist:

  1. The op-graph IR (catopt_core.ir) — what the sinks lower, what the cost model prices, what the certificate replays against.
  2. The arrow calculus — lab/bridge/combinator.py today, handl's Term + Language in the direction ADR 0010 adopts.

ADR 0008 warned that two peers drift: every law must exist in both, or one becomes second-class. ADR 0009 deferred the question ("decide the canonical representation — its own ADR") rather than answering it. The spike has now measured the facts the decision needs:

  • Q0 (PASS) — catopt's vocabulary is handl data: a Language assembles from catopt-emitted fragments; a law lands in lang.transforms with its fields intact; the catopt: extension field is ignored by handl's serde and therefore rides along losslessly. Two representational requirements were found and fixed (fold when into And([...]); spell enum variants as YAML tags).
  • Q1a (yes) — a tensor crosses by reference: TaggedValue::Resource(Rc<ForeignResource>) is an opaque host pointer with a finalizer. No copy, no JSON. So tensor semantics can live outside the kernel entirely.

Decision ​

Two representations, but not peers: each is canonical for a different question, and the bridge carries an authoritative record so neither is a lossy view of the other.

  1. The calculus is canonical for rewriting. A law is a TransformDef row (data) — the user-facing rule "laws are exclusively data" lands here. Guards, conditions and derivations are the calculus's business; handl's rewrite/typecheck engines read this representation. Rationale: the engines that matter for discovery read it, and it is the representation that makes laws diffable, versionable and mintable.
  2. The op-graph IR is canonical for execution. Lowering, cost, verification against the source, and every executor (generic, batched, fused, graph, Triton) speak the op-graph. Rationale: the sinks are written against it, and Q1a shows tensors are host resources — there is nothing to gain by making the calculus carry execution, and a great deal to lose (every sink would need a second implementation).
  3. The catopt: extension field is the authority. The bridge already states this ("lossless by construction"): the handl-shaped fields are a structural view, the catopt field is the record, and cell_from_handl reconstructs the cell exactly. So the two representations cannot drift silently — a drift is a failed round-trip, which is testable.
  4. Execution stays catopt's; rewriting becomes handl's. handl's exec engines are an interpretation of handl's terms (used by its rewrite and typecheck passes); tensor execution stays in catopt's executors. This is not the "fallback" of ADR 0010 — it is the decision: the kernel is the language and its rewrite machinery, not the tensor runtime.
  5. Laws must be expressible in the calculus (the data-only direction), with the procedural-hook count as the ratchet.

Falsification ​

  1. The bridge's refusal rate is the metric. If laws cannot be expressed in the calculus vocabulary, the calculus is not canonical for laws. The first measured gap is already known: catopt's leaf step ({"catopt:leaf": …}) has no counterpart in handl's RewriteStep. If that gap cannot be mapped (candidate: a Created node carrying the value in its payload, or Fresh(hint) for variables), then laws with literal leaves cannot be data, and the decision above is wrong for them.
  2. Round-trip is the seam test. store → handl → cell must be identity on the shipped bank; a failure is a drift, not a detail.
  3. If execution ever needs the calculus, the decision in (2) is wrong — but that must be shown by a measured need (a form the op-graph cannot express), not by symmetry.
  4. Data-only must not raise the procedural-hook count (ADR 0010's ratchet). A law that cannot be data stays procedural and is counted.

Honest scars ​

  • This decision is only as good as Q1b. Q1a shows the channel exists (a resource variant); it does not yet show a tensor op crossing it end-to-end through exec. If Q1b fails, the calculus still cannot run anything tensor-shaped, and (4) becomes the split by necessity rather than by choice.
  • The type of a resource is by convention. Nothing in handl distinguishes a tensor from a file descriptor; the host must.
  • Two representations still cost something. The bridge, its tests and the round-trip discipline are permanent overhead. They are worth it only because the alternative — laws in Python and the calculus as a notation — is what the project is trying to leave behind.
  • The op-graph's authority for execution is contingent on the sinks. If a future backend speaks terms natively, (2) should be revisited.

Consequences ​

  • "Laws are exclusively data" becomes implementable with a defined home: the calculus, loaded as fragments, with the catopt record as the authority.
  • The e-graph is one strategy over the calculus's laws, not the architecture.
  • The kernel does not need to run tensors — which is what makes ADR 0010's "handl is the kernel" cheap: no tensor runtime to port, no second set of executors.
  • The seam has one test: round-trip identity on the shipped bank.

Outcome (spike, 2026-10-14) ​

The decision is now backed by measurements rather than symmetry:

  • The calculus side works for the whole bank. 83 laws load as TransformDef rows, and catopt's own seam test proves the bridge is lossless in both directions on the shipped bank (no cell silently dropped; every row re-emits byte-identically). So "laws are data in the calculus" is not aspiration — it is the current behaviour of the bridge, one mapping away from being the only representation.
  • The op-graph side is untouched and needs to be: no measurement asked execution to move, and Q1b showed the boundary is JSON-valued, so moving it would buy nothing and cost every sink a second implementation.
  • The authority rule held: the catopt: extension is ignored by handl's serde and round-trips exactly, which is what makes the two representations non-drifting by construction rather than by discipline.
  • The named refusal metric moved: the leaf gap (the bridge's first measured refusal) is closed; the leaf now maps to !Fresh or a !Create of a declared kind.
