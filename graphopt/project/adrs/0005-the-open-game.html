ADR 0005 — The open game: human-free discovery inside anchored anchors ​

Status: accepted — phase-1 gate passed; the next surface is planned (plan 0024). Date: 2026-10 (updated 2026-10-15). Depends on: ADR 0002 (the thesis), ADR 0003 (the four dimensions), ADR 0004 (abstraction as move). Related: catopt_discovery.cellstore (the store everything is), catopt_discovery.genkernel (the runtime minter), plan 0017 (serialization), the tier registry.

Context ​

ADR 0004 opened the move set: constructed objects — folds, bridges, carriers — are admissible data, not pre-approved Python. Since then the machinery closed a demonstrable loop: on torch.export(ChunkedRetention), equality saturation under a guarded law set produces decay, decay_vec, and folded applyd/aff_diag carriers unaided; the scan tier lowers those carriers into real triton.jit kernels verified to ~1e-16 on CUDA; minted composites land in the cell store as op cells the registry walks uniformly.

The pressure this ADR answers: who authors the rules of play? Today the honest surface is threefold — the axiom the alphabet lacks (each missing structure was one guarded R(...)), the guard/derive predicates inside it, and the candidate enumerator. If those stay human, the system is an optimizer with a very good closure engine; it is not the optimizer that discovers. The question is which pieces of the game are anchors — things that cannot be self-minted without voiding the game — and which are merely current capabilities.

The measured boundary so far:

  1. Closure is machine-owned. Given the vocabulary, the e-graph composes, folds, verifies, extracts, and lowers without naming anything — the RetNet recurrence is the existence proof.

  2. Invention is partially machine-owned. Composite ops mint today (declare emits op+law+handle cells); handlers mint (generated Triton runtimes); verified candidate equivalences mint (the compose sweep admits on a small space).

  3. Guards, enumeration, and derives are now partially machine-owned (updated 2026-10-15):

    • guards synthesize — auto_cond_object mints the smallest declarative cond covering the measured-equal sites; the game's first conditional law (xy + xz = x(y+z) iff x ≡ y) came from it, not from a human;
    • candidates enumerate — catopt_discovery.enumerate instantiates 27 rewriting forms over the op grammar (1,160 candidates at depth ≤2, five paying laws auto-found), and open_game plays the stream as a board whose learned prior beats random on held-out pages;
    • derives synthesize — dspec_synth mints a declarative dspec from a procedural hook's measured outputs, refusing (with the missing bank op named) when the grammar cannot express it.

    What remains human is the form list (the 27 skeletons) and the mint target (which atom to propose) — one level up, planned as plan 0024. ADR 0004's standing lesson — direct law invention measured ~10× worse than structured construction — applies unchanged.

  4. The alphabet is open, not closed. A minted composite is new vocabulary; a declared op + generated kernel is an invented atom whose semantics is its code, verified observationally. The floor is substrate-expressibility, not the shipping op list.

Decision ​

The optimizer is an open game: every rule, object, vocabulary, runtime, and pricing inside it is machine-made data admitted by anchored machinery — and the only fixed pieces are the substrate, the correctness oracle, and the clock.

Concretely:

  1. Three anchors, nothing else. (a) The substrate — torch, GPU, the Triton compiler; the machine cannot mint the thing it runs on. (b) The correctness oracle — observational equivalence of outputs on real tensors plus certificate replay; a machine that could redefine equivalence voids every certificate, so the checker is machinery, not mintable data. (c) The measured objective — wall-clock time and memory on the substrate; a self-minted objective is a rigged race. Everything else — laws, guards, ops, carriers, runtimes, cost models, the proposal grammar — is inside the game.
  2. The VM picture is already the architecture. Ops are machine descriptions (op cells); handlers are machine instantiations (handle cells); the codegen tiers emit the runtimes (generated kernels are small minted VMs); carriers are state machines (applyd = h ← a⊙h+x). A cost model is a handler into flop/byte space — therefore mintable; only the measured referee stays anchored.
  3. The alphabet stays open by construction. New vocabulary enters two ways: composites (closed-form — decay_vec = pow(γ, arange), retractible as a declared atom) and declared-op/generated-kernel atoms (open-form — semantics is the emitted code, verified observationally). The residual floor is whatever the substrate cannot express — universal computation is in reach; silicon is not.
  4. The learned prior guides a verified search — RL does not replace it. The verifier is perfect (exact + observational, no fuzzy reward), the cell store gives curriculum, and imitation on claiming traces already warm-starts the policy. What RL does not fix: candidate density (~10⁹ law shapes, most invalid), cold start, and credit assignment across pattern + guard + derive. Guard synthesis is constraint-solving — conds are already data — not a policy problem. Quality-diversity beats raw RL for frontier coverage.
  5. The four dimensions hold at the meta level too. A synthesized law/guard/runtime is a search artifact that must pass the same semantics gate a shipped R(...) passes — numeric oracle → typed-pay gate → certificate replay → adversarial sweep of the guard's false region. Admission is never a vote the machine casts for itself.

The categorical spine — why the game is well-posed ​

The ontology is the theory of why this architecture works; the machinery computes equational closure regardless. Its content:

  1. Legality is derivable, not chosen. A move must be a structure-preserving map — folds, bridges, carriers are homomorphisms, the unique maps out of free structure. The proposer's space is defined by the algebra's shape, not a policy's imagination.
  2. Correctness is a formal object. Equivalence = same morphism in the semantic category; the e-graph is congruence closure — the free category modulo equations. A certificate is an algebraic proof the checker replays, not a heuristic.
  3. Engine-genericity is interpretation-as-functor. Evaluate, cost, verify, lower are the same fold to different target algebras — one traversal, four purposes. The four-dimension split (ADR 0003) is the consequence, not the choice.
  4. Chunking is a theorem, not a trick. fold(concat x y) = fold x ◇ fold y — the scan monoid homomorphism — is why the derived RetNet recurrence is exact. The machine produced the fold; the theorem guarantees legality. No tensor-level optimizer can state this claim.
  5. Knowledge is structural, so it transfers. Laws are equations on morphisms; carriers are state-machine morphisms; costs are denotations into ℝ. A minted cell carries structure, not tensors — which is why admitted knowledge applies to the next model unchanged.

The honest caveat: this is CT's shallow end — Lawvere theories, equational closure, monoidal structure. No 2-cells, no string-diagram machinery, and the code never invokes it. Its role: the language the theorems are written in — what the game may mint, what admission certifies, and the formal spine any publishable claim stands on.

Consequences ​

  • The repository shrinks to machinery + the declaration kernel. Per-architecture knowledge — axioms, carriers, bespoke handlers — migrates into generated cells; dead knowledge dies in the evidence store without deprecation. The codebase gets fewer abstractions, not none: one generic game replaces N hand-authored optimizations.
  • The measured win is the phase-1 gate. Nothing is defensible until a minted carrier beats the incumbent on a real model. Passed (2026-10-15): on the spelled ChunkedRetention export at T=512, extraction prefers the derived chunked carrier (29.4M vs 39.1M FLOPs) and the end-to-end probe measures 1.82–2.33× against the fused baseline (866–1113 µs vs 2018–2028 µs), fp64 max-abs ≈1.3e-8. Honest scope: the paying kernels are the decayed fused + elementwise ones; the scan kernels are correctly declined as unloweable. The K-way construct (om_kway_laws) then cut the extracted cost a further 4.4× (6.70M, fold depth 1→7), with the O(T·C) floor (~2.59M) approached but not reached — extraction still prefers the spelled cross form.
  • Guard synthesis is the research bet. If proposer + constraint-synthesized guards produce non-trivial admissions at useful rates, the claim is real — the rule set as a synthesized, measured, admitted object is the novel contribution. If not, the system remains: humans write axioms, machines compute closure — a solid optimizer, not the vision.
  • Honest scars carried forward (updated 2026-10-15; closed items struck through). Post-saturation site binding (closed — leaf_env travels harvest → handler → profitable, and a raising kernel now declines instead of aborting the sweep); derive=-as-data (closed where the grammar reaches — dspec_synth mints specs from measured bindings, refusals naming the missing bank op); candidate enumeration and the learned prior (built — enumerate + open_game; the prior is now trained and baked and its ordering beats the cost-delta heuristic on held-out pages, AP 0.133 vs 0.045). Open: extraction prefers the folded carrier (the ~2.59M floor); the form list and the mint target are still human (plan 0024); the falsified naive-RL path — enumerated, not reopened.
  • What this is not. Not RL solving search; not an LLM writing rewrites; not a new op list. It is a game whose fixed pieces are silicon, the oracle, and the clock — the claim is human-free optimization discovery within a declared algebra.

Status of this ADR ​

Accepted (2026-10-15) — the phase-1 measurement (carrier vs fused on a real export at large T) passed: 1.82–2.33× at T=512, with extraction preferring the derived carrier. The research bet (proposer + constraint-synthesized guards) has its first witnesses: the game minted a conditional law and the synthesizer names its own gaps. The open question has moved up a level — the form list and the mint target, planned as 0024.

The architecture's fixed pieces are settled; what remains is the admission rate of the synthesized surface at the form level, which only evidence settles.
