Invariants (violating one is a failure regardless of results) ​

  1. No force-push; no remote-branch deletion without explicit consent.
  2. No rm -rf outside regenerable caches (~/.cache, venvs for dead worktrees).
  3. No widening of any ratchet (coverage floors, ty excludes, ruff ignores, radon baseline, the procedural-hook count).
  4. One heavy suite at a time; check free -m before each; abort below 2.5 GB available.
  5. No invented numbers. A number that cannot be sourced is marked UNSOURCED or dropped with the reason.
  6. Nothing irreversible without asking (destructive ops, remote state, other repos' history).
  7. Leave the tree committed and pushed, with docs/md updated in both repos — so the next session starts from a clean, documented state.

If something fails ​

The ledger shows which goal is unmet and why — no silent drops. Salvage beats abandon: uncommitted work in a dying worktree gets committed to its branch before anything else.
